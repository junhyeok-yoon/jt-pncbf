## 0. Seed basis, scoring basis, and how to read this document

**Seed basis: single seed, 42, on every cell of this version without exception.** No cell was run at
a second seed and no aggregate over seeds exists. Every interval in this document is therefore a
single-run interval, and every verdict is flagged single-seed.

**Two interval sources appear, and each is labelled where it is used.**

1. `cps_ci` as persisted by the evaluator inside a score artifact. Used wherever it exists.
2. A scene bootstrap recomputed for this document from a persisted per-episode array, where the
   artifact persists no interval for the quantity in question. Its resample count and generator
   seed are stated at the point of use.

Numbers taken from a per-episode array that the artifact does not summarise are marked "recomputed"
at the point of use.

### 0.1 The scoring basis

`cps` is defined at `src/eval/evaluate.py:480-486`:

    cps_episode = reach - 2.0*collision - stuck - 0.5*(oob + timeout) - 0.3*infeasible_step_frac

`cps` and `cps_v2` are two columns of `docs/ledger.md` carrying the same quantity under this
version's cell; `scripts/check_ledger.py:366-369` requires them to be both present or both blank,
and on every v2.9.3 row they are equal. Where this document says `cps` for a ledger row, both
columns hold that value.

The admissibility floor referenced throughout is **0.0083**, from `docs/protocol/04_eval.md:347-348`:
it is the measured cross-batch-size spread at a fixed checkpoint, and no `cps` claim below it is
admissible regardless of its confidence interval.

## 2. Versus motivation

The version opened without a registered axis, so there is no stated motivation to score this record
against. What can be done instead is to say, for each of the questions the version's dispatches
actually pursued, what came back — and to be explicit that these are the questions as reconstructed
from the build-logs and the ledger, not from a `changes.md` that was never written.

### 2.1 The four questions the version spent itself on

**1. Does joint training need a warm certificate?** Answered: **no separation at a matched budget on
any of four systems** (§6.3, §7.4). Four intervals, all covering zero. On two systems the point
estimate is admissibly large and negative, so the finding is a failure to separate rather than a
demonstration of equality. **This is the version's largest single body of evidence and it is a null.**

**2. Can the penalty route reach the certificate route?** Answered: **no, across four stages and
nineteen scored settings** (§7.2). The best point the penalty route produced anywhere is `cps`
0.71425, against the pair's 0.89025 on the same pool and cap. Two dense costs built to give the
penalty route the certificate's own per-step signal both scored **below** the sparse cost, which
falsifies the premise those stages were run on.

**3. Is there an analytic baseline on the 3-D quadrotor?** Answered: **yes, and it is far behind**
(§7.1.2, §7.26). A depth-four HOCBF cascade reaches `cps` −0.22522, better than the depth-two
cascade's −0.56695 and 0.42 above the unfiltered nominal, still 1.12 below the pair. It never
declines to enter — incomplete 0.0000 at every one of twenty-one gains over 0.2 to 180 — and its
supply falls short of its demand on 0.7428 of live steps under the constant-term definition.

**4. Which pool, which cap, and which checkpoint is the manuscript on?** Answered, and this consumed
more of the version than any single experiment (§7.13, §7.14, §7.15, §7.16, §7.23, §7.28). The
basis is `fullvia` at 400 steps on the cold-ablation checkpoint. Getting there required building a
pool that did not exist, re-scoring nine rows onto it, finding that three backup-filter rows had
moved for a **horizon defect and not a pool effect**, and rebuilding the policy-alone cell.

### 2.2 What the version did not do

- **It set no axis** (§1) and wrote no `changes.md`.
- **It ran one seed.** Every row, every interval, every verdict. No second seed was run or proposed.
- **It bolded nothing and promoted nothing.** The four bold rows in §8 were bold before this
  document was written and are unchanged by it.
- **It left twenty-four `n` = 2000 evaluations without a ledger row**, of which **seventeen still
  carry none** (§12.5) — the seven PPO-Lagrangian frontier points were registered on 2026-08-30 as
  L390-L396. Each build-log records a decision not to propose a row, which `06_workflow` §2.5
  requires be recorded; the seventeen are absent all the same, and §12.5 lists them against the tasks
  that produced them.

### 2.3 The honest summary

**The version is a large, careful, single-seed measurement campaign that closed several axes
negatively and set none.** Its most reusable products are not results but bases: the `fullvia` pool,
the 400-step cap, the cold-ablation checkpoint, the depth-four analytic baseline, and the reproduction
gates that tie them together. Its most cited number will be `cps` 0.89025 on L372, which is a
re-scoring of a checkpoint this version trained onto a pool this version built. **Its interval,
[+0.86799, +0.91274], is disjoint from every baseline interval in §6.1** — from the certificate-free
baseline's [+0.69925, +0.77226], from OC-PNCBF's [+0.69216, +0.76037], from both backup-QP rows'
[+0.77344, +0.83527] and [+0.77661, +0.83972], and from R4's [+0.67458, +0.74585] — and lies wholly
above both analytic conditions' point values, neither of which carries an interval. **What it does
not separate from is the rest of its own family**: L369 and L370, the warm and cold-at-40000
checkpoints of the same method, whose intervals it overlaps almost entirely. An earlier draft said it
"separates from nothing except the two analytic conditions", which was wrong in both directions.

### 4.1 What this version closed, and is therefore not suggested

  without a resolvable effect, not closed against.**

### 4.2 The three that one experiment each would resolve

**A. A second seed on the basis-of-record cell.** Every verdict in this document is single-seed, and
§3 cannot claim an improvement without one. This is the cheapest item on the list and the one that
most changes what the version can say: one 40000-step run of the cold-ablation configuration at a
second seed, scored on `fullvia` at 400 steps against L372. **It resolves nothing about the method
and everything about whether the method's numbers mean anything**, which is why it is first.

**B. The budget-versus-buffer-cap confound.** The +0.01453 gain from 10000 to 40000 steps moves two
training keys at once and no artifact in the repository separates them (§12, D11). One run — 40000
steps at `buffer_cap` 1000000, if memory permits, or 10000 steps at 200000, which certainly does —
turns a confounded contrast into a clean one. The v2.9.0 `BUFCAP` series suggests the cap's own sign
runs *against* the budget explanation, which makes the experiment worth more than its cost.

#### 7.1.4 Why the depth-four filter never declines to enter

**Question.** The cell records `incomplete` 0.0000 — no stuck, no timeout, no out of bounds. The

### 7.12 What the `c_gain` axis reaches

**Question.** The cold ablation of §7.5 moves one key, `env.quadrotor_3d.c_gain`. Exactly which term
of the hazard does that key control, and does zeroing it make the hazard position-only.

**Cell and basis.** Source reading, no run, no ledger row.

**The answer is a bounded scope claim, and it is negative.** `c_gain` is read at exactly one line,
`src/common/quadrotor_barrier.py:115`, where it multiplies the **horizontal** approach term
`v_xy · r_hat`. The complete list of `c_gain` readers in `src/` is that one line.

**Zeroing `c_gain` therefore removes the horizontal approach term and leaves both vertical velocity
terms in place.** The vertical band branches carry `+/- c_z v_z` with `c_z` computed from
`omega_max`, and **no configuration key sets `c_z`**. So `c_gain` 0.0 alone does **not** give a
position-only hazard on the 3-D quadrotor.

Per system, from the same source read: the double integrator and the unicycle carry **no** `c_gain`
key at all and take `h = phi` directly; the planar quadrotor has **one** velocity term, `v^T Re`, the
projection of velocity on the rotating body thrust axis, which resolves into neither world-horizontal
nor world-vertical; the 3-D quadrotor has **three** velocity terms, of which `c_gain` reaches one.

**Verdict: the ablation of §7.5 is a horizontal-term ablation, not a position-only hazard.** The
position-only condition needs a second key and is a separate line of work, §7.22. This correction
governs how L363 may be described, and it is the reason §7.22 exists.

### 7.30 The margin probe: what binding steps have in common

**Question.** Does the hazard's saturation floor bound the α(V̂) slack, so that the filter's
constraint sits near-active far from every obstacle? Registered as a hypothesis under test in the
artifact's own `hypothesis_under_test` field.

**Cell and basis.** The registered L363 `fullvia` h400 cell, checkpoint step 31500, pool sha
recorded in the artifact, `ebs` 2000, `v282_agree_gate.gate_overrides`. **Instrumentation only** —
the shipped `HardNetFilter` is wrapped and no filter mathematics is touched; the reproduction check
passed 2000 of 2000 outcomes against the registered row. Artifacts
`data/runs/v2.9.3/margin_probe/margin_probe.json` and `margin_active_steps.npz`. Producer
`scripts/analysis/v293_margin_empty.py`. Build-log
`docs/versions/v2.9.3/fig1_v2_margin_empty_verify.md`, Tracks A and B. **No ledger row.**

**Definitions**, from the artifact: `a = L_g V̂`; `b = −L_f V̂ − α(V̂)·V̂`, the shipped `_row_upper`;
**margin = b − aᵀu_nom**, and margin ≤ 0 exactly when the nominal input violates the row.

was predicted to cause is not.

#### 7.30.2 Track B — what the binding steps actually share

Recomputed from `margin_active_steps.npz`, seven parallel arrays of length 150157.

- **Binding is a `V̂` ≈ 0 phenomenon, not a clearance one.** Median `V̂` is **−0.03671** on binding
  steps against **−0.67960** on non-binding ones. Restricted to binding steps with clearance beyond
  4 m — 493 of them — the median `V̂` is −0.03583, **indistinguishable from binding generally**.
- **0.29671 of active steps have `V̂` > −0.1, and 0.76047 of those bind.**
- `‖a‖` median is 0.31001 binding against 0.21619 non-binding.
- **EMPTY is a strict subset of binding**: **every** EMPTY step binds, and **no** non-binding step is

**Verdict: the filter binds where the certificate is undecided, not where the geometry is tight.**
The two coincide often — a certificate near zero is usually near an obstacle — but the ladder above
separates them, and it is `V̂` and not clearance that predicts binding at every clearance. This is a
diagnostic and carries no ledger row.
