## Part III — Mechanism findings (the yields)

1. Task-conditioned V smuggles liveness. Decoupling the value-target conditioning policy
   removes label drift and cuts collision ~2x, but exposes that the policy cannot detour
   on its own (the task cost has a rest local minimum when forward progress is filtered;
   the payoff of a detour lies at/beyond the 1.5 s BPTT window). The baseline's low stuck
   is the filter steering, not the policy navigating.
2. The BPTT-horizon collapse is caused by differentiating through the projection's
   state-dependent CBF coefficients (h-Hessian via _cbf_terms, the alpha 2->100 jump at
   h=0, box-argmin geometry) at filter-active steps, compounding multiplicatively over the
   chain (per-step Lyapunov 0.19; per-sample grad max ~17x per horizon doubling; top-1%
   batch-gradient share 0.21 / 0.51 / 0.87 at T=15/30/60). The new flag
   loss.policy.detach_filter_coeffs removes the tail (max 143x down in ablation; max 8.2
   in training) while preserving median/p90. The same spikes exist in v2.3.0 (T=30, top-1%
   share 0.51): the baseline has been training at the edge of this instability, absorbed
   by grad clipping. The flag is a standing hygiene option, default off.
3. The infeasibility floor is value-side-invariant: 0.154 (v2.3.0) / 0.147 (Step 1) /
   0.132 (Step 2) across three conditioning policies, after buffer (v2.3.0) and coverage
   (v2.3.1) axes were already flat. Decomposition: singular (||L_g h|| < 5e-4) 0.086-0.119
   dominates empty-intersection 0.036-0.049. Step 5 — a policy-side change (T=60 +
   coefficient detach) — is the first variant to move it within the same attribution
   series: infeas 0.077 at the adopted checkpoint (singular 0.035, empty 0.041), at the
   cost of stuck; direct evidence that the floor responds to policy visitation, here as an
   incidental effect of the T=60 behavior rather than a targeted mechanism. Policy-side
   visitation is the targeted lever of v2.4.1; the singular component is a relative-degree
   property outside any first-order-filter fix.
4. Latent baseline defects registered (present in v2.3.0, unchanged here): ~52-55% of the
   BPTT return integrates past the first physically-terminal event (goal/collision/oob,
   equal at T30/T60); wrap_state leaves position unbounded; SUM (return) vs per-step MEAN
   (regularizers) scale imbalance; the reach-tolerance parking cliff; the reserved
   cps-floor/grad halts are unwired (the Step 3/4 collapse class is invisible to the wired
   halts).
