## 0. Seed basis, scoring basis, and how to read this document

**Seed basis: single seed, 42, on every cell of this version without exception.** No cell was
run at a second seed and no aggregate over seeds exists. Every interval in this document is
therefore a **single-run scene-bootstrap** interval per `06_workflow` §2.6 part 1, not a
pooled multi-seed interval, and every verdict is flagged single-seed.

**Two interval sources appear, and they are labelled wherever they are used.**

1. `cps_ci` as persisted by the evaluator inside the score artifacts. Used wherever it exists.
2. A scene-bootstrap recomputed for this document from the persisted per-episode arrays —
   10 000 resamples of the 2 000 episodes with replacement, percentile method, generator seed
   `20292`. Used for component shares, for the collision split, and for **paired**
   differences, none of which the artifacts persist an interval for.

The two agree where they overlap: on the four cells where both exist the recomputed `cps`
interval reproduces the persisted `cps_ci` to within **0.0023** at both endpoints.

**Scoring basis.** Two episode caps appear in this version and the distinction runs through
everything below.

- **The registered basis, `eval.max_steps` = 200.** The cell the registered axis was
  designed against, and the basis on which its falsifier is scored: the registered
  `v282_agree_gate.gate_overrides` cell, pool per system, `n` 2000, `eval_batch_size` 2000,

## 2. Versus motivation

`changes.md` registers one hypothesis and one falsifier.

> **Hypothesis.** The approach term `c·(v_xy · r_hat)` restores first-order horizontal
> authority to a position-form hazard; with `c_gain = 0` the horizontal channel loses it while
> the vertical band terms are untouched.
>
> **Registered observable A1**, on the registered cell: `coll_obstacle`. **FALSIFIED if
> `coll_obstacle` at c_gain 0 fails to exceed its parent's (L305 / L312) on either [condition].**
> `coll_band_lower` and `coll_band_upper` are reported beside it and carry no registered
> verdict; if they move, the manuscript claim narrows.

### 2.1 A1 — NOT falsified, on both conditions

The falsifier is a **directional** test on a single registered observable, and it is met on
both conditions:

- **OC condition:** `coll_obstacle` 0.0310 → **0.0500**. Exceeds. 62 → 100 episodes, +38.
- **JT condition:** `coll_obstacle` 0.0075 → **0.0090**. Exceeds. 15 → 18 episodes, +3.

**A1 is not falsified. The registered hypothesis survives on the observable it registered.**

### 2.2 What the margins are actually worth, stated plainly

The two conditions do not carry the same evidential weight and the document would mislead if it
recorded only "not falsified".

- **The OC condition is a clean effect.** The paired `coll_obstacle` difference is **+0.0190**
  with 95% CI **[+0.0095, +0.0280]** — excludes zero, and its lower endpoint is above the
  0.0083 admissibility floor. The obstacle channel is the only collision channel that moves:
  `coll_band_lower` is **identical**, 53 episodes on both sides, paired Δ exactly 0.0000,
  and `coll_band_upper` is 0 on both. That is the hypothesis's own prediction in its sharpest
  possible form — the horizontal channel degrades and the vertical band channel is untouched
  to the episode.
- **The JT condition is directionally right and inside noise.** The paired `coll_obstacle`
  difference is **+0.0015**, three episodes, 95% CI **[−0.0020, +0.0050]** — **crosses zero**,
  and the whole interval sits below the admissibility floor. The falsifier as written asks
  only whether the share exceeds, and it does; it does not ask for separation. **On the JT condition
  the registered observable moved by an amount this seed basis cannot distinguish from zero.**

### 2.3 What moved instead, on the JT condition

`cps` falls on both conditions and the fall is separated from zero on both: OC **−0.0624**
[−0.0922, −0.0325], JT **−0.0184** [−0.0351, −0.0022]. On the JT condition that fall is **not**
carried by collision — collision is flat at 0.0250 vs 0.0255 — it is carried by reach
(−0.0065), by timeout (+0.0055), and above all by **infeasibility**, which rises 0.0792 →
0.1082, and by the `SINGULAR` episode fraction, which rises 0.0955 → **0.2485**. On the OC
condition `SINGULAR` rises 0.1675 → **0.3955**.

That is the mechanism the hypothesis names, arriving through a different observable than the
one registered: removing the approach term does not primarily add collisions on the jointly
trained condition, it **removes rows from the constraint**, and the certificate goes singular
**2.36× as often on the OC condition** (0.1675 → 0.3955) and **2.60× as often on the JT
condition** (0.0955 → 0.2485). `EMPTY` does not move with it (§1.1): the deployed feasible set is
no emptier, the rows that define it are fewer. The registered observable caught this on the OC condition, where
the policy is fixed and cannot compensate; on the JT condition the learned policy absorbs the loss
of authority into infeasibility and time rather than into contact.

`coll_band_lower` moved on the JT condition — 0.0180 → 0.0160, paired Δ −0.0020, CI
[−0.0040, −0.0005], excludes zero but an order below the floor. Per the registration's own
clause, this narrows the manuscript claim: the band channel is **not** strictly untouched on
the jointly trained condition, and the "vertical band terms are untouched" half of the hypothesis
holds exactly on the OC condition and approximately on the JT condition.

## 4. Next axis — a suggestion, not a commitment

The data of this version points at three candidates, in the order the evidence supports them.

1. **The singular clause, as an axis rather than as bookkeeping.** §7.4.3 measures that the
   deployed infeasibility term disagrees with `04_eval` §1 on **every one of 20 cells**, and
   that the disagreement is **14 % to 84 %** of the term. Six row-families shift by more than
   the admissibility floor when the protocol definition is used, the largest being **+0.0347**
   on L329 — four times the floor. This is the largest single unresolved quantity in the
   scoring stack and it moves every registered row, which is exactly why it is an axis and not
   a correction.
2. **Singularity as the JT condition's real failure mode.** §2.3 shows the ablation's cost on the
   jointly trained condition arriving as `SINGULAR` 0.0955 → 0.2485 rather than as collisions, and
   §7.7 shows the certificate probe separating the two frameworks on exactly this axis: the JT
   certificate's row norm falls below threshold on **24 of 24** probe scenes against the OC
   certificate's **7 of 24**. A hazard or an architecture change aimed at row conditioning has
   a measured target.
3. **The band channel on the jointly trained condition.** `coll_band_lower` is the one collision
   channel that is not flat, and it is the channel the v2.9.1 axis already failed to move.
   Two versions have now touched it without separating it.

Not suggested: another `c_gain` value. The axis is answered — the term does what the
hypothesis says, and the OC condition measures it cleanly.

disagreement, not a decision to resolve it. §13.1 states what would close it.

#### 7.7c Explicit null — the two singular measures are not comparable

The version reports two quantities both called a singular fraction, and they order the two
frameworks **oppositely**:

| measure | axis | OC | JT | ordering |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| xy-slice rows below threshold (§7.7, `item_c`) | state-space volume, one attitude and one velocity, on a 121² grid | 0.00000 (p50), 7 of 24 scenes | 0.01506 (p50), 24 of 24 scenes | **JT higher** |
| `SINGULAR` episode fraction (§1.1, L305/L312) | any-step occupancy on realized trajectories | 0.1675 | 0.0955 | **OC higher** |

**This is a null, not a contradiction, and neither number corrects the other.** They measure
different things. The slice fraction asks what share of a *hypothetical position grid* has a
degenerate constraint row when attitude and velocity are pinned at hover and rest; the episode
fraction asks on what share of *actually flown episodes* a degenerate row was met at least once,
at whatever attitudes and velocities the closed loop visited. A certificate can be degenerate over
a large volume the deployed policy never enters, and non-degenerate over a small volume it enters
constantly. §7.8 shows the second effect directly: singular steps occur at a median tilt of 1.36°
on the OC condition against 8.17° on the JT condition, while the OC condition's *live* steps sit at
a median tilt of 3.43° — so the OC condition spends its time near the attitude where its own
degeneracy lives, and the hover-and-rest slice is a poor proxy for that.

**Consequence for reading this document.** No sentence anywhere may compare a slice fraction with
an episode fraction, and the two are never differenced. The framework separation of §7.7 rests on
