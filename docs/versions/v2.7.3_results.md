# v2.7.3 — results

Verdicts on the hypotheses registered in `docs/versions/v2.7.3/changes.md` before any data was seen.
Evidence is in `phase_v273_report.md` and the five v2.7.3 ledger rows — four eval rows plus the M3
value run, added retroactively so that every training run has a trace in the ledger. Status:
measurement complete through M7; CLOSE-PENDING on git and on the one protocol edit the adoption rule
calls for.

## Verdicts

**H-diff — CONFIRMED, at the band edge.** Pre-JT nominal+HardNet collision on the re-registered pool is
0.0305, inside the registered [0.03, 0.08] and above the 0.020 triviality floor. The margin is thin:
the projection from the v2.7.2 pass-through ratio predicted 0.027, and the observed value sits between
that and the band. Read this as "the re-registration bought signal", not as "it bought a lot".

**H1' — CONFIRMED.** JT canonical cps 0.8573 [0.8318, 0.8792] against pre-JT 0.6794 [0.6454, 0.7124].
The intervals are separated with room to spare, on the same frozen pool and the same plant, batch-2000.
Collision falls 0.0305 to 0.021 and reach rises 0.8635 to 0.941, so the gain is not a trade of one
against the other.

**H-fallback-3D — CONFIRMED on all three clauses, and the adoption rule fires.** With the k=5
empty-branch fallback: collision 0.0065 [0.003, 0.010] against 0.021 [0.015, 0.0275], CI-separated
below; all 108 outcome flips fall in empty-branch episodes, with both collision partitions at
`had_empty_frac` 1.0; and there is no conservative paralysis — reach improves to 0.9605 [0.952, 0.968],
CI-separated ABOVE the none arm, with timeout overlapping. The rule therefore points to adopting
`kstep k=5` as the deployed 3-D default. **Action deferred: the `02_control` edit is a close action and
has not been applied.**

The reach result deserves emphasis because it is the opposite of the usual failure mode. Strengthening
a safety filter normally costs progress. Here it bought progress, which says the none-arm's residual
collisions were not the price of reaching the goal but a loss of both at once.

## What this version established

**The trial itself is the achievement.** v2.7.2 recorded the same k=5 mechanism as a scaling
measurement and explicitly could not adjudicate it: 19 residual collisions is not enough to separate
anything. v2.7.3 was built to fix that, and it did — empty-branch rose to 2.99% of active filter steps,
and the trial resolved cleanly. The difficulty re-registration was not an end in itself; this is what
it was for.

**The actuator model is now physical.** Control is the per-rotor thrust box `[0, 4.905]^4` with a fixed
mixer, which is the true actuator constraint rather than a wrench-space box that permitted 1.0 N*m of
torque at zero collective thrust. The new admissible set is 1.364% of the old box's volume, and 0.144%
of it falls outside the old box, entirely in `tau_x` and `tau_y`. Yaw authority, previously about 6x
the physical maximum, is now exact at 0.157 N*m. Measured cost at the nominal: none — nominal-only
collision moved 0.358 to 0.354, inside noise at n=500 — because the cascaded-PD never visits the region
where the two sets differ. The correction should therefore bind on the filtered arm, not the nominal.

**Two axes, and the attribution limit that follows.** This version changed both the actuator set and
the scene distribution. Every hypothesis above is a within-version contrast on one pool and one plant,
so none of them is confounded. But no v2.7.3 number may be compared to a v2.7.2 number, and the
individual contribution of the two changes is not separated and was never designed to be.

## Findings not registered in advance

**A calibration proxy does not transfer between axes.** M1 registered `frac>=1` in [0.50, 0.75] and it
saturated near 0.5 regardless of obstacle count. The cause is the scene acceptance rule acting as
negative feedback on difficulty: raising density raises rejection specifically for segment-blocking
scenes, 0.76 to 1.61 per accepted scene, so raw values of 0.576 to 0.758 are pressed down to 0.328 to
0.468. The band lay outside the reachable set of the well-posed scene family, which makes it a
specification error rather than a result. It was retired and replaced by the property it stood for,
measured directly: collision given blocked 0.8868 [0.8443, 0.9292] against 0.2083 [0.1597, 0.2569] when
not blocked, ratio 4.26, with 75.8% of all collisions in the blocked stratum. The difficulty is
navigational, not IC-driven. This same failure recurred in v2.7.4 M1 with a different proxy — a
nominal-only collision band, insensitive because nominal collision is saturated by geometry — so it is
a pattern and belongs in the protocol.

**The bptt_T=60 collapse was horizon, not depth.** The gradient probe run at close separates the two
factors the original experiment confounded. Policy-gradient magnitude collapses onto the physical
horizon `H = T*dt`: 22.6, 87.9, 429.3 at H of 0.75, 1.5, 3.0 seconds. At fixed horizon, going 2.5x
deeper ATTENUATES it — 429.3 to 257.5 at H = 3.0, a factor 0.60, with the attenuation growing with
horizon (0.91, 0.79, 0.60). This is what `detach_filter_coeffs` implies: with the filter coefficients
held constant in backward, the filter's backward path is a projection onto a convex set and is
non-expansive, so additional depth can only shrink the gradient, never amplify it. The practical
consequence is that dt 0.05 -> 0.02 at T = 75 is not a risk: its gradient norm, 69.3, is below the
current configuration's 87.9.

**The residual concentrates where authority is weakest.** At the 42 residual collisions, median
`||L_g V_hat||` is 0.196 with 21.4% below 1e-3, against a healthy distribution elsewhere. Residual
failure is a low-authority phenomenon, which is consistent with the fallback fixing 30 of the 42 and
with every flip being an empty-branch episode.

## What is not established

Single seed, 42. No multi-seed claim is made and none was registered.

Cost is unaddressed. The adopted fallback costs 37x wall clock, 1068.7 s against 28.6 s. It fires on
about 3% of steps, so the fallback itself is roughly three orders of magnitude more expensive per
invocation — |G| = 25 candidates times a 5-step lookahead. Whether that fits a real-time budget is
unmeasured and is the deployment question, not a result here.

The commanded control is not smooth, by construction: the projection switches active sets and the
fallback selects from a 25-point grid. Under a first-order motor lag the cost of that scales with the
per-step command swing, up to 43% of a rotor's full-scale mean thrust for a full-scale swing. The
action stream was persisted at M6 so this is computable, but it has not been computed.

`saturation_rate` 0.4106 from v2.7.2 was in wrench coordinates and is not commensurable with anything
measured here.
