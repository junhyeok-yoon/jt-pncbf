## 2. Axis 1 — sigma-augmented hazard (FAILED; its registered primary is now scored)

### 2.1 What it was

The certificate is input-blind: `A = L_g V̂`, `b = -L_f V̂ - alpha·V̂` is built with no knowledge of
what the box can supply, so the box/half-space intersection can be empty. The axis put the deficit
into the **label**: `h~ = max(h, beta·psi(sigma(x)))` with `sigma` the row deficit, so the value learns
to be positive where the box cannot meet the demand. Registered primary, both legs: empty share DOWN
and `u_safe` switching share DOWN vs CTRL at matched step. Guard: cps not regressing.

the entire −0.0105 gap and more.

**This generalizes past sigma.** Any label-side or value-side repair moves `V̂`; the demand follows it
through `alpha·V̂`; the empty branch does not close. The loop is broken only by changing the **row**.
The seventh axis therefore names the eighth.

## 14. Process lessons recorded

- **In-loop leads are not results.** Sigma led 17 of 18 in-loop evals and lost the final; the in-loop
  pool is easier and consecutive evals of one run are strongly autocorrelated.
- **Eval-only discrimination before any training axis**, predictions and falsifier registered before
  data, and a hard-min control arm wherever a smoothing constant exists. Box-Bellman was filtered in
  minutes; six earlier axes each cost a training run to reach the same kind of answer. §4.1 adds the
  converse lesson: **the discrimination's own verdict must be re-read when the cell changes.**
- **A gating metric is qualified before it gates** (04_eval §5). The sigma primary failed the metric-
  exists check on the comparator and could not be scored for the whole version.
- **An ablation row is not a baseline.** R2 borrows the project's own policy; the pure baseline is R4.
- **Undeclared defaults redefine cells** (penalty → 10.0; `goal_angrate_radius` absent → infinity;
  `eval.in_loop.n` unread; unknown `filter.*` keys silently dropped). Read effective values back off
  constructed objects — the practice that made §1.1 trustworthy.
- **Selection rules degenerate at extremes.** Constrained selection, threshold declared before data.
- **Two independent harnesses agreeing to the digit is the strongest evidence available** for a
  scoring path (D1 ≡ R1, 14 fields plus the collision-scene list).

## 15. Next axis (suggestion, not a commitment)

The empty branch is the only surviving dead spot, and §2.3 shows why the row — not the value and not
the label — is where it must be repaired: any change to `V̂` moves the demand `alpha·V̂` with it. The
candidate clamps demand to supply at row construction, in the single row constructor
(`src/common/filter_hardnet.py:583` `_row_upper` `[V]`), leaving `V̂`, the label, the losses and the
exact `dual_solve` projection untouched.

Three items are owed before that axis is registered, and two of them change its shape:

1. **The clamped row's feasibility characterisation** (Strategist theory item, delivered): the
   multiplicative clamp's residual empty set is `{shipped-empty} ∩ {s < 0}` and is invariant in the
   clamp constant, so the constant cannot be tuned to reduce empty; an **additive retreat** closes the
   branch globally and retains a positive-dimensional feasible set. This changes the candidate form.
2. **Forward-invariance character under a clamped row** (Strategist theory item, delivered): the
   certified set's invariance survives exactly where it is achievable at all, and the shipped row
   enforces nothing on the complement.
3. **The flag definition.** Flag invariance (02_control §4) requires the infeasibility flag to keep
   firing on the *original* condition `alpha·V̂ > s`, not on clamp-bind; otherwise `cps` is not
   comparable across the axis.

### 15.1 What the successor version's measurements have established so far

Recorded here because they bear directly on the next axis and were measured on this version's assets.

- **Every collision episode passes through the empty branch.** On `fullscr41`, active-window empty
  steps in collision episodes number 398 at 7.804 per episode over 51 episodes `[V]`, and R1' records
  exactly 51 collision episodes (0.0255 x 2000) `[V]`. Necessary, not sufficient — goal episodes carry
  8436 such steps. The alignment is arithmetic and wants one direct confirmation before it is quoted.
- **`s < 0` is a transient, not a doom set.** Restricted to the active window, the coupled bound is
  margin-negative on 2.18% / 0.77% of `s<0` states `[V]`, not the 51% / 14% measured over the
  unrestricted capture. The apparent conflict between the plant-side and bound-side reads was the
  capture defect propagating, not a disagreement about the plant.
- **The provably unescapable subset is small and terminal.** 85 / 28 states, of which 83.5% / 89.3%
  sit in collision episodes against a 2.1% / 1.6% base rate, and they lie 1–5 steps from termination
  `[V]`. Margin-negative states inside goal episodes lie 56–95 steps from termination and the episode
  recovers. By the time a trajectory is in the unescapable subset the outcome is already decided.
- **The horizon objection is inert on this population**: the fixed-vs-remaining-horizon sign join is
  perfectly diagonal `[V]`. The inadmissibility remains a property of the code, unrepaired.

Taken together: the addressable set is the escapable empty states inside collision episodes — those on
which an admissible escape exists, the filter has no valid action, and the episode collides anyway.

### 15.2 The order of work this implies

A deployment-side eval-only pass on `fullcb` — clamp forms (multiplicative and additive), fallback
horizon `k`, and the V_dep layer scored side by side on `cps` and collision-by-class — precedes any
training registration. `route_qual` measured the fallback's argmin moving on 42–77% of empty steps as

rather than its subject. Nothing is registered against it: the version's own record is that six axes
