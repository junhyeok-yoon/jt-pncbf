## 1. Result

v2.5.2 opened as the unicycle port of the exact-backup safety chain and, mid-version, ran the
J-arc diagnostic that redirected the whole v2.5.x line. Two distinct results.

**Unicycle port (training, filter-only).** Seeds 42, 99 completed to `n_steps = 50000`
(`halt_reason` null). In-loop `cps` best: seed 42 `+0.6457` @40500, seed 99 `+0.6765` @42000;
no new best after step 42000 (plateau). Reach ~0.89–0.91, collision ~0.000–0.002 at verified
points. **No full-pool dual-arm (A/B/B') evaluation was run**, so the registered predictions
P-U1..P-U7 — including the HALT-class P-U1 (arm-B verified-start collision = 0) — are
**unscored**. Seed 12345 was intentionally not launched. cps is therefore an in-loop figure,
not a verdict-grade full-pool result, and the unicycle safety claim is **not empirically closed**.

**J-arc (eval-only, DI, v2.3.0 learned filter + S′ shield).** Per-seed and pooled A/B/B' from
`phase_j_report.md`. Gate verdicts: P-J0 PASS (arm A reproduces v2.3.0: pooled `cps_legacy`
0.8739 vs 0.8698, `cps_v2` 0.8992 vs 0.9005), **P-J1 PASS (verified-start collisions = 0, every
seed, arms B/B')**, P-J2 PASS (arm-B pooled `cps_v2` 0.9171 > 0.8422), P-J3 PASS, **P-J4
FALSIFIED** (arm-B `overrides_per_ep` = 0.489, not > 1.0).

Comparator baselines: LQR-only unicycle `cps_v2` −0.0115 [−0.071, 0.051]; secured v2.3.0 DI
`cps_v2` 0.9005 (P-J0 reference).

## 2. Versus motivation

§2.1 hypothesized that the exact-backup safety chain would transfer to a nonholonomic system via
one brake primitive, and — implicitly, as the v2.5.x thesis — that the analytic barrier was the
right response to the v2.4 trilemma. The data splits these.

- **Port mechanics: confirmed.** The chain (barrier + filter + shield) ported to the unicycle;
  Stage 1 passed its gates and two seeds trained to budget without halt. The *machinery* transfers.
- **The v2.5.x thesis: refuted by J-arc.** J-arc shows the S′ shield's zero-collision guarantee is
  **provenance-independent**: it holds on the v2.3.0 *learned* filter (P-J1), delivering pooled
  `cps_v2` 0.9171 with collision 0 — above every analytic-barrier result of the line (v2.5.0
  0.8327, v2.5.1 0.8160). P-J4's falsification (override rate 0.489, not high) shows the learned
  filter already keeps the policy largely backup-safe, so the exact barrier bought little. The
  analytic barrier was solving a problem the shield already solved, at a cost of ~0.08 `cps_v2`.

## 3. Improvement verdict

**No improvement of record for this version; the version's value is diagnostic.**

- Unicycle: **not verdict-gradable** — no full-pool dual-arm eval, single deployment arm, 2 of 3
  seeds. Establishes a unicycle in-loop baseline only. Not comparable across systems to DI
  (`06_workflow` §2.4: cps not commensurable across systems).
- J-arc: the highest `cps_v2` recorded in the project (0.9171, DI, collision 0), but a **different
  deployment class** (learned filter + shield) than the standing DI SOTA basis — not SOTA-bolded;
  flagged for Researcher classification. The DI SOTA of record (v2.3.0, 0.9005) is unchanged.

The genuine result is negative-for-the-line and positive-for-the-direction: **the v2.5.x
analytic/shield-first path does not beat a jointly-trained learned CBF, and the shield transfers
onto that learned CBF for free.**

## 4. Next axis

The data points back to the project's core thesis — **learned OC-PNCBF + value–policy joint
training** — now targeted at high-dimensional *underactuated* systems where a hand-designed CBF
has high relative degree. A companion theory note (`docs/proposals/`) establishes: (i) the PNCBF
value collapses the relative degree to one; (ii) on the active safety boundary the residual
first-order degeneracy is confined to $B_0=\{V=0\}\cap\{h=0\}$; (iii) a single scalar barrier
augmentation $h_\star=\phi(p,o)+c\,(v^\top Re)$ removes it, making joint training well posed on
the planar and spatial quadrotor. This is v2.6.0. Note the direct-filtering requirement (HardNet
on $(f_{\mathrm{thr}},\tau)$, not PD-mapped accelerations) — a departure from the existing
quadrotor abstraction.

Not carried forward: the analytic single-backup barrier, the S′ shield as a *headline*
mechanism (it remains a provenance-independent safety wrapper, but is not the contribution), and
the unicycle exact-backup arc (its dual-arm closure is optional, not a prerequisite for v2.6.0).

Suggestion, not commitment.
