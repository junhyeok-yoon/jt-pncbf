**Single axis.**" Mechanism check — "The share of collected training states whose label sits exactly at

### 7.4 The exponential-hazard recomputation — the form, not the value

*The close fact-gather recorded `data/runs/v2.8.4/exp_hazard/` as the one run directory with no matching
build-log and left this section an explicit empty placeholder. `docs/versions/v2.8.4/exp_hazard.md` is
since confirmed to exist and to have been read (§14.1), and this subsection is written from it and from the
two v2.8.5 documents that quote it section by section. Numbers reaching this document only through those
quotations are marked with a dagger (†).*

**What it asked.** §7.3 establishes that the deployed ramp is blind by arithmetic beyond its width and that
the width was never screened. That leaves two distinct questions: *what value* the width should take, and
*what functional form* should carry it. `ramp_width` answered the first as far as recomputation can; this
study asks the second, on the same two `morphology` captures, at the same fidelity, over ten derived length
scales — `derived_ells_used` = [0.0585, 0.0975, 0.1044, 0.1581, 0.174, 0.2098, 0.2188, 0.2636, 0.3647,
0.504] — with two independent passes written to `pass1/` and `pass2/` at identical sizes.

**Provenance and scope, inherited from §7.3 and stated again because it binds identically.** It is
arithmetic on recorded states under a frozen policy. It bounds the conservatism cost of a form **only if
the policy behaves identically**, and the entire point of a wider hazard is that it should not. This is
registered as open problem (O6) in `theory.tex`. **No threshold and no falsifier is registered on any figure
below.**

**The form.** $\phi_{\exp}(d) = 2e^{-d/\ell} - 1$ for $d \ge 0$, clamped above at $+1$ for $d < 0$ — the
$+1$ saturation inside the cylinder **retained**, so the label clamp and the value ceiling see the same
upper endpoint they see today, and the final $\mathrm{clamp}(\cdot, -1, 1)$ is untouched.

**Geometry — the three quantities cannot be matched simultaneously** (†):

| | contact-face slope $-2/\ell$ (m⁻¹) | α switch surface $\ell\ln 2$ (m) | $\phi = -0.9$ (m) | $\phi = -0.99$ (m) |

1. **The observation ceiling** (ITEM 0(e) †). Under the deployed `hard_topk`, $K = 5$ encoder, the distance

**Rung by rung.**

- **L1, iterations.** Monotonically **worse** under the shipped cost: 1.0560 → 1.2089 → 1.3655 → 1.6762 →
  1.9389 at 1/2/3/5/8 iterations. The per-iteration diagnostics say why: at ESS p50 ≈ 930 of 1024 the
  weights are near-uniform, so **five iterations are five independent random-walk steps of the nominal, not
  five refinements** — the increment ‖ΔU‖ p50 is flat to three digits across the iteration index
  (0.5514 / 0.5525 / 0.5483 / 0.5594 / 0.5368). **But the mechanism is not the iterations**: under the
  reference cost the same five iterations *do* refine — `L1_L2`'s increment decays 8.84 → 6.01, a **32 %
  contraction**, and its argmin-cost sample reaches p50 0.154 m. "L1's idling is a property of the shipped
  cost's resolved temperature, not of the iteration mechanism."
- **L2, cost form.** The largest single-rung improvement, 1.0560 → 0.2962. Two mechanisms, both measured.
  (i) **A temperature effect, not only a cost effect** — ESS p50 collapses 846.9 → **1.7**, because our
  `c_crash` is 1e5 against their `w_obstacle` 13.56, so under the same relative-λ rule the reference cost
  resolves a temperature ~3 orders sharper; `L2_L4` (0.5967) is **worse** than either alone, which is what
  double-sharpening looks like. (ii) **The transcribed thrust term charges for existing** —
  `w_thrust · thrust²` at thrust ≈ mg is **1.635 per step** against `w_goal · d²` = 2.18 at 3 m and 0.242
  at 1 m; measured cost share **0.413 thrust vs 0.539 goal**. Consequence: `L1_L2` resolves **stuck** at
  speed p50 0.404 m/s, 1.047 m short — station-keeping is cheaper than closing the last metre. Measuring
  the same term from hover instead (labelled an **interpretation**) drops the thrust share to 0.039 and the
  closest approach to 0.236 m, and the stuck outcome disappears.
  The predicate was **provably untouched**: the reference cost needs `I_crash` and `I_height` separately,
  and both legs are read out by **calling** the shipped `cost.collision_mask` twice, with
  `obstacle_leg | band_leg == collision_mask(...)` asserted at every physical rollout step — **8000 checks
  per cell, 0 mismatches**.
- **L3, horizon 60.** Essentially inert, 1.0560 → 1.0151 — consistent with the settled fact that horizon
  coverage is not binding ($H\,dt\,v_{\max} = 5.0$ m at H = 40 against a 3.0 m traverse).
- **L4, temperature convention.** The shipped rule is **relative** (`lam_eff = lam_rel · std_k(C_k)`,
  `cascade_rate.py:698-703`); the absolute branch already exists at `:694-697`, so L4 is a
  construction-time selection at $\lambda = 1/8.054 = 0.1242$ and **adds no code**. Result 1.0560 → 0.2896
  at ESS p50 exactly 1.0000. This does **not** contradict the settled "forcing ESS to 1.0 makes it worse
  (1.145 m)": that figure changed two things at once (i.i.d. noise **and** absolute λ) at the config's own
  λ = 0.05. Both are reported.
- **L5, proposal quality.** Colored / low-frequency noise (Vlahov et al., RA-L 2024) implemented; log-MPPI's
  normal-log-normal mixture **not**, and the reason is a measurement rather than a preference — the sample
  set is **not tail-starved** (§8.10 diagnostic below), so thickening marginal tails targets a
  non-binding constraint. The unrun branch exists in the code. Result: alone 1.0153 (exp 2) / 0.7628
  (exp 4); on top of L1–L4, **0.9181 → 0.0836**, crossing the position-only radius for the first time.
- **L6, weight tuning** (last, and only because L1–L5 did not reach). 16 random draws plus draw 0 (the
  untouched reference point), 3 held-out obstacle-free scenes disjoint from the gate, 52 episodes,
  seeded random search inside the repo (no dependency installed). **Two things it measured matter more
  than the selected point.** (i) **The success term of the reference's own objective is identically zero
  across all 52 episodes**, so the search reduced to $5\times$ position $+\,2\times$ orientation error and
  was **structurally blind to the settling conjunction the gate tests** — transplanting their tuning
  objective transplants a criterion that cannot see our terminal. (ii) **The selected point does not
  transfer**: on the gate scene it scores 0.5899 m against the untouched transplant's 0.0836 m at the same
  configuration, **seven times worse**. Reported as measured rather than re-run until it agrees.

> **Does.** With `K_seed = 0`, our cascaded $(T, \omega)$ MPPI does not satisfy our deployed reach predicate

### 8.11 The $(T, \tau)$ variant — implemented, measured, reported separately, NOT advanced

It makes the interface comparison exact — sampling $(T, \tau)$ is one level below our $(T, \omega)$ — and it

decision step and the degenerate rule holds the nominal — at $|z|$ 12.5 m. **Not a usable configuration**,
reported as measured.
