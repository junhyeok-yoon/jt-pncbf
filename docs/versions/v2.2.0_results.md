# v2.2.0 Results — Deploy-Time Failure Diagnosis: Detection, Mechanism, and the Located Value-Function Fix

**Version scope.** v2.2.0 is a **measurement-and-diagnosis-only** version: no change to the control law, safety filter, training procedure, or cps. The deployed filter at eval is bit-identical to v2.1.0. SOTA is therefore unchanged: JT DI single-seed **cps 0.8853** (seed 42, step 34000, N=500; the bolded ledger row reads 0.8852 — a 0.0001 index-vs-ledger rounding discrepancy, immaterial), multi-seed mean **cps 0.8777 ± 0.0074** over seeds 42/12345/99 (pooled 95% CI [0.8373, 0.9152]).

**What v2.2.0 set out to do, and what it found.** The version began as an attempt to *decompose* deploy-time failures using a learned-value epistemic-uncertainty signal (SCOD). Over 19 read-only diagnostics it became a forensic investigation that progressively overturned its own working hypotheses and converged on a single, concretely-located root cause. The headline conclusions:

1. **The epistemic (SCOD) axis has no practical value.** The CBF-consistency residual dominates it on both failure detection AND failure-type classification. SCOD is, at best, a redundant type-axis; the residual alone is the usable signal.
2. **Collision is not temporally preventable at deploy time, but stuck is.** Even with a perfect detector, the genuinely-preventable collision ceiling is ~18.4% (collisions are detected ~7 steps before impact, too late); stuck is flagged ~44 steps before the physical stall.
3. **The learned nominal is LQR-like.** It is goal-straight with avoidance outsourced to the CBF; JT's premise of a steering nominal is not realized as steering.
4. **The analytic HOCBF is an oracle/diagnostic standard, not a deployment replacement** — and, honestly, in *this* setup (circular obstacles, known positions via partial-exact observation, known dynamics) the analytic CBF being viable is a real limitation on the learning value's marginal contribution.
5. **Collisions are 100% a learned-value control-authority (forward-invariance) failure.** `L_g h = ∂h/∂v` is ~5× weaker than the analytic CBF at short clearance; V_S alarms correctly and early but cannot brake.
6. **α_unsafe is NOT a lever.** Lowering it strictly worsens collisions (feasibility ≠ safety). The entire collision fix falls to the value function.
7. **The value loss lacks any CBF-derivative term.** It is pure MSE-to-PNCBF-target; `∂h/∂v` is never directly constrained, and the HardNet-filtered collection under-samples the short-clearance fast-approach states where `∂h/∂v` is weakest.

The located fix — **add a CBF-derivative auxiliary term concentrated on short-clearance fast-approach states, and collect those states with velocity diversity** (both oracle-free) — is carried forward to v2.2.1. v2.2.0 designs no fix.

## 0. Substrate and conventions (orientation_audit, changes)

**Lineage.** PNCBF (So et al., ICRA 2024) → OC-PNCBF (value conditioned on the obstacle field; nominal still LQR) → **JT** (the project's primary contribution: learns the nominal *through* the differentiable HardNet filter so safety and performance gradients meet at one differentiable point). The known JT value-collapse risk: a good policy+filter produces rollouts that carry little unsafe signal → the policy-conditioned value collapses → the filter goes inactive. This recurring failure mode reappears as a *contributing* cause of the velocity under-realization (§7).

**System (DI).** State (px, py, vx, vy) dim 4; action (ax, ay) with per-component |a_i| ≤ u_max = 2.0; dt = 0.05; obs dim 19, translation-invariant `obs = [vx, vy, rel_gx, rel_gy, then per nearest-5 obstacle (rel_cx, rel_cy, r)]`, Top-K = 5. Only obs[0:2] (= v) depend on velocity, so `L_g h = ∂h/∂v = grad_x(h)[2:4]` via the chain rule, and `∂h/∂p = grad_x(h)[0:2]`. JT deploys through HardNet; OC through CBF-QP. The value net is a 2-member ensemble (each 3-layer MLP, hidden 256, Softplus β=20, output clamped to [−1,1]; the Softplus is structural — the PNCBF theorem assumes C¹ and HardNet divides by ‖L_g V‖² which ReLU dead neurons can zero); deployed `h` = ensemble mean, target `h` = ensemble min (Polyak EMA τ=0.005). ~273k params.

**Canonical metric.** `cps = reach − 2·collision − stuck − 0.5·(oob + timeout) − 0.3·infeasibility`.

**Stuck predicate.** For t ≥ W_stuck, stuck fires when the max displacement in the trailing window from an anchor W steps ago ≤ r_stuck (defaults W_stuck = 60 = 3 s at dt 0.05, r_stuck = 0.10). Priority lock collision → goal → oob → stuck; **stuck does not terminate the rollout**, and the window-trip step (persisted as `n_steps`) **lags the physical stall by up to W = 60 steps** — the physical-stall onset is not stored (it is reconstructed as trip − 60 where needed).

**Signal availability.** HardNet returns only (u_safe, infeasible = singular | empty_intersection); h, L_f h, L_g h, ‖L_g h‖, α, b = −L_f h − α·h, A·u_nom are all computed then **discarded** each step. The committed eval persists only aggregates; per-step signals are recomputed by a forward pass on stored states (the `jt_failure_dissect` recompute pattern, verified bit-identical throughout this version). **Deployed `h` = mean ensemble is the operative signal** (the eval loader loads only v_s_state + pi_state — the target/min head is not available at eval).

**What v2.2.0 added (changes).** Two per-step axes over the committed full pool, with **NO config delta** to training/eval/control: (i) a grade-2 SCOD epistemic-uncertainty axis for V_S (last-layer Fisher, 2-member), and (ii) a CBF-consistency residual axis, `residual = relu(h_dot_safe + α·h) = cbf_violation_safe` (avoid-sign). The initial hypothesis: collision concentrates where SCOD is high; planning-trap stuck sits in the both-axes-low-yet-failed region.

## 1. The SCOD / detection investigation (stage0 → diagnostic → stage1 → stage2_decomposition → detection_rates → confusion_3x3)

This series asked whether a learned-value epistemic signal can decompose and detect deploy-time failures. It progressively **demoted SCOD** and **promoted the CBF residual**, then showed even the residual cannot prevent collisions.

### 1.1 Biased existence check (stage0_reliability_probe)
On a biased sample (positives = saved failures: 26 collision, 105 stuck; negatives = 120 matched goal controls; reconstruction max mismatch 2.98e-08), **both axes carry failure association**. Episode-AUROC: epistemic (2-member |v1−v2|) collision **0.954** / stuck 0.577; residual (cbf_violation_safe) collision **1.000** / stuck 0.912. The **differential** (collision − stuck) is large for epistemic (gap 0.377) and small for residual (0.088) — i.e. epistemic looks like a *type* axis. The "reliable-yet-failed" (low-low) region — the V_S-confident-but-failed signature — concentrates in **planning-trap stuck (103/105 episodes at timestep level)**, essentially absent in collision (24/26 have ≥1 low-low step but driven by box-infeasible mechanism). *Caveat:* biased sample (existence, not detector rates); the residual median is degenerate (0) because 0.767 of goal episodes have zero CBF violation.

### 1.2 Is the epistemic axis a dead end? (scod_failure_diagnostic)
The faithful grade-2 SCOD (saved Fisher online closure, σ_out = 0.18190, ε = 1, k = 21/23; reconstruction mismatch 0.0) **captures real collisions comparably to the 2-member proxy** on the biased sample: episode-AUROC SCOD collision **0.9564** vs 2-member 0.9545 (gap −0.0019, SCOD slightly exceeds). Critically, **collisions are in-manifold low-density near-obstacle-tail states**: 25/26 have surface distance < 0.10 (median 0.026), and surface distance < 0.10 is only **0.53% of rollout states**. This **refutes** the alternative "SCOD is insufficient because it misses in-manifold low-density collisions the 2-member catches." *But* the reference choice moves the number materially (collision-vs-goal-timestep AUROC 0.755; goal-control episode-max 0.956; start-state probe 0.687), and SCOD is *below chance* on stuck (0.44).

### 1.3 The reversal (stage1_scod_decomposition)
With a principled SCOD (exact 257-param last-layer Fisher per member; OOD gate **PASSED** — big_radius 0.9245, centers_outside 0.9949, fast_velocity 0.9911, all ≥ 0.75; near_obstacle 0.848 under the correct deployment-manifold reference vs 0.687 under the start-state reference; more_obstacles 0.684 excluded as obs-invariant under Top-K=5) and an **unbiased** N=500 pool, the picture **reverses**: SCOD collision-AUROC **0.8941** is the *weakest* of the three (2-member 0.9802, residual 1.0000). SCOD retains the **largest differential** (0.380) — it is the type-distinguishing axis. Low-low concentrates in stuck (12/16 episodes) with planning-trap geometry (clearance median 0.210, closing speed median 0.000). *Caveat:* only 4 collisions at N=500 → high variance.

### 1.4 Adequate-N confirmation (stage2_decomposition)
At N=30000 (deployment distribution; **283 collision, 1198 stuck, 304 timeout, 1000 goal-reference**; overall goal 94.05%, failure 5.95%), the reversal **holds with non-overlapping CIs**: collision-AUROC **residual 1.000 > 2-member 0.988 > SCOD 0.903**. SCOD has the **largest differential gap (0.482)**. The 2×2 (SCOD median 7.65, residual median 0.000 degenerate): collision is **100% SCOD-high + residual-high**; planning-trap stuck concentrate **SCOD-low** (765/768 of the SCOD-low stuck are geometric-trap); the literal **low-low quadrant is EMPTY** because near-obstacle stuck always violate the residual (so the "reliable-yet-failed" signature survives on the *epistemic* axis only). Collision is foreshadowed ~42–46 steps ahead by SCOD/residual but **0 steps** by the 2-member proxy. Mechanism labels: collision **98.6% box-infeasible-cbf-constraint** (layer 84.1% fixable-upstream-policy, 15.9% dynamics-doomed); stuck **98.0% near-obstacle-projection-saturation-trap** (layer 85.5% planning-limit-geometric-trap, 14.5% fixable-chatter).

### 1.5 Detection rates at usable operating points (stage2_detection_rates)
A high AUROC does not imply a usable TPR. At a fixed **5% goal-FPR**: residual catches **100% collision / 87.3% stuck**; 2-member 92.2% / 18.4%; **SCOD only 37.8% / 1.2%**. SCOD's 0.90 collision-AUROC does *not* translate to TPR at low FPR — the distributions overlap in the tail. Type accuracy (SCOD ≥ 7.65 ⇒ collision) is 71% (collision 100%, but stuck 64.1% — 430 SCOD-high stuck mistyped as collision, 409 of them near-obstacle-projection-saturation-trap).

### 1.6 The decisive ablation — residual carries both axes (stage2_confusion_3x3)
The user-specified two-filter design (residual → success/failure, SCOD → collision/stuck) gives accuracy 0.767 with **type-error 0.278**. But the **residual also types near-perfectly by magnitude**: collision residual [min 49.9, median 92.6] vs stuck [max 56.9, median 3.8], only **1/1198 overlap**. The **residual-only** classifier (residual to detect at 5%-FPR + residual-magnitude to type at threshold 49.9) is the joint-error minimizer: **accuracy 0.916, type-error 0.0008** — adding SCOD as the type filter strictly *introduces* error. SCOD-only cannot detect (miss rate 0.918). The physical reason: collision = box-infeasible = huge CBF violation; stuck = oscillation = small violation; violation **magnitude** separates the three classes, so SCOD has no practical role. *Caveat:* accuracy is sample-specific (over-weights failures vs ~94% goal at deploy); per-class recall is composition-independent.

### 1.7 Detection ≠ intervention (stage2_leadtime)
Even with the residual as a clean detector, **collision detect-then-intervene is not temporally feasible**. At 5%-FPR the collision lead is median **7 steps** [5,10] (flagged-at-impact rate 1.000, only 22.6% reach ≥11-step lead); applying a physical-avoidability gate (required decel = closing_speed² / (2·clearance) ≤ u_max) to the lead ≥ 11 subset gives **52/283 ≈ 18.4% genuinely-preventable ceiling** (detection-moment median clearance 0.70, closing speed 0.013). **Stuck inverts**: the residual flags **99.7%** of stuck a median **+44 steps before** the physical stall onset. SCOD adds no earliness for collision (subset-matched lead identical to residual) and only marginal, low-coverage earliness for stuck (+13 steps on the 148-episode both-flag subset). *Type-spike caveat:* 109/1198 stuck (9.1%) transiently exceed the type threshold (would stream as mistyped collision, ~91% correct streaming).

**SCOD-series conclusion.** The residual is the practical signal — it detects (100% collision / 87% stuck at 5%-FPR) and types (residual-only accuracy 0.916) better than any SCOD combination. SCOD is at best a redundant type-axis. And detection alone cannot solve the problem: collisions are detected too late to prevent. The investigation therefore pivots from *detecting* failures to the *mechanism* that produces them.

## 2. The failure mechanism (stage2_policy_obstacle_response, stage2_stuck_mechanism, stage2_l2qp_compare)

### 2.1 The learned nominal is LQR-like (stage2_policy_obstacle_response)
Recomputing u_nom on all stored states (reconstruction max abs 6.9e-6): the nominal does **not** bend with obstacle geometry. The angle(u_nom, ĝ) is **14.6° when an obstacle is in-path** vs 17.2° when not — a gap of **−2.6°** (robust across thresholds; the residual perpendicular deflection ~21.5° is velocity-damping, not obstacle-driven). At **88.2% of near-obstacle steps the nominal points toward the obstacle** (u_nom · outward median −1.114); the CBF supplies the outward push (median 55% of outward motion). The nominal is goal-straight in **both** success (u_nom · ĝ +1.424) and stuck (+1.438); the only difference is CBF activity (angle(u_nom, u_safe) 0.013° when geometry permits vs 84.1° in the stuck phase). The obs-perturbation sensitivity ratio obstacle/goal = **0.46** (~2× more goal-sensitive). **Verdict:** the learned nominal is qualitatively LQR-like goal-seeking with avoidance outsourced to the CBF; JT's premise of a steering nominal is *not realized as steering*. Stuck is not a special policy failure — it is the same goal-straight policy meeting geometry where the CBF cancellation traps rather than deflects.

### 2.2 Stuck = CBF-binding progress cancellation (stage2_stuck_mechanism)
Pure algebra on the stored CBF row over 1198 stuck episodes (71,520 stuck-phase steps; feasibility by box∩halfspace vertex enumeration): the nominal aims at the goal every step (u_nom · ĝ median **+1.44**, positive 100% of steps) but the binding CBF row (tight in 77%) projects it to a **saturated (86.9%), ~84°-rotated braking** action (u_safe · ĝ median **+0.18**, speed median 0.128). Category split: CBF-binding progress-cancellation **77.0%**, nominal-collapse **0.0%**, box-saturation-only 10.3%. **90.0% of episodes are single-step escapable** (a CBF+box-feasible goalward action exists that the filter isn't taking); the feasible direction is **tangential** (best-u angle to goal median 60°, to obstacle tangent 34°; 65% of escapable steps more tangential than goalward). A tangentially-aimed nominal would recover u_safe · ĝ 1.00 vs 0.72 goal-aimed. **10.0% of episodes are blocked** (goal direction CBF-forbidden in 94.9%, 55.3% with empty box∩{A·u≤b}). *Caveat:* single-step escapable is necessary, not sufficient — the real trap is an oscillatory cycle between escapable and blocked states.

### 2.3 The projector is not the cause (stage2_l2qp_compare)
Comparing HardNet to the exact Euclidean CBF-QP (KKT active-set, cross-checked vs cvxpy max abs diff 8.9e-16) over the same stuck states: HardNet **over-corrects** — ‖u_qp − u_safe‖ median 1.759, rotation **75.8° vs L2-QP 34.6°**, intervention ‖u − u_nom‖ HardNet 2.298 vs QP 1.187 (paired HardNet − QP +0.906). The over-correction (and the bulk of the apparent 84° rotation) is a HardNet artifact: its box-aware candidate set **omits the box-edge perpendicular-foot face**. **BUT goal-progress cancellation is a genuine CBF-constraint consequence, not a HardNet artifact**: the paired goal-progress difference (QP − HardNet) is **≈0** (mean +0.019, and ≈0 even on escapable steps). **This falsifies "swap HardNet → L2-QP would resolve stuck"** — the exact QP recovers no goal progress. The lever for stuck remains re-aiming the nominal, not changing the projector. *Caveat:* per-step open-loop on HardNet-visited states; closed-loop swap not run here (it is run in §3).

## 3. The HOCBF oracle: isolating filter vs nominal (stage2_hocbf_comparison, stage2_hocbf_deploy_n2000)

To separate "is the failure the filter or the nominal?", v2.2.0 built an **analytic** higher-order CBF as an *oracle/diagnostic standard* — explicitly NOT a learned method and NOT a deployment replacement.

**Construction (filter_hocbf.py).** DI is relative-degree-2 in the geometric safety h0 = ‖p−c‖² − r_safe²: per obstacle, ψ1 = 2(p−c)·v + α1·h0, enforcing ψ1̇ + α2·ψ1 ≥ 0 gives a control-affine row 2(p−c)·u ≥ −[…]. One row per nearest-K obstacle + box, solved as a joint exact 2-var QP. Verified: row ≡ CBF condition 4.5e-13; QP vs cvxpy 1.2e-12. **Gains α1 = α2 = 2.0**, confirmed by a researcher-inspected 4×4 trajectory grid (clean tangential rounding, clearance 0.092, 131 steps) and a 4×4 numeric sweep (17/25 gains reach 12/12 with 0 stuck anywhere; α1=8,α2=8 collides; α1=0.5,α2=0.5 times out — a competent, not strawman, baseline). r_margin = 0.05, K = 5. Obs-equivalence verified exact (surface-dist and radius diff 0.0): the HOCBF consumes precisely the obs nearest-5 channels — **no privileged scene info**.

### 3.1 On stuck scenes (stage2_hocbf_comparison)
Single-obstacle collinear (27 scenes, both nominals × both filters): **all 27/27 reach, 0 stuck** — a lone in-path obstacle traps neither filter; **stuck is a multi-obstacle phenomenon**. On the real Stage-2 stuck scenes (PNCBF re-run reproduces stuck 898/1198 = 75%, bit-identical first ~50 steps then chaotic float-noise divergence, outcome-robust), swapping PNCBF → HOCBF under the **same learned nominal** resolves **441/898 = 49.1% to goal with 0 collisions**; 442 remain stuck. The advantage is **not** from seeing more obstacles (K=5 → 610 goal ≈ K=12 → 615). Velocity profile (idx 11): where PNCBF stucks (mean v·ĝ 0.117, stays 4.29 m out) HOCBF reaches (v·ĝ 0.509, final dist 0.065) — **PNCBF kills forward progress; HOCBF preserves it and curves around**. **Verdict:** the stuck trap is partly filter-specific, partly nominal-inherent — a better CBF resolves ~half; the rest needs an obstacle-aware nominal.

### 3.2 As a deployment filter (stage2_hocbf_deploy_n2000)
Over a full success-included N=2000 pool (seed 20260617), swapping PNCBF → HOCBF (same nominal, same V_S-derived policy, same scenes; only the filter differs):

| filter | goal | collision | stuck | timeout | **cps** | infeasibility |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| PNCBF (HardNet) | 1867 | 23 | 86 | 24 | **0.8258** | 0.1188 |
| HOCBF (K=5) | 1855 | **0** | 133 | 12 | **0.8580** | 7.8e-5 |
| Δ | −12 | −23 | +47 | −12 | **+0.0321** | −0.1188 |

Δcps **+0.0321**, paired 95% CI [+0.0070, +0.0567] (excludes 0). **But the gain is safety + an infeasibility-accounting term, not stuck resolution**: HOCBF eliminates all 23 collisions and removes the infeasibility penalty, yet **stucks more** (86 → 133) and reaches slightly fewer (1867 → 1855). **Ex-infeasibility, PNCBF marginally wins** (0.8615 vs 0.8580) — the full-cps flip is entirely the infeasibility term, and PNCBF's infeasibility is **~69% non-intervening / ~72% >0.5 m from any obstacle** (benign singular-gradient pass-through). Paired contingency: goal→stuck **71** (dominant regression: HOCBF's myopic over-conservatism pins agents at the r+0.05 margin), stuck→goal 40, collision→goal 14, timeout→goal 13; n_improved 78, n_regressed 89.

**Honest reading (a recorded judgment, not a number).** In *this* setup — circular obstacles, known positions via partial-exact observation, known dynamics — a properly-tuned analytic HOCBF is collision-free by construction and at task-outcome near-parity with the learned PNCBF, which makes the learning value's *marginal* contribution genuinely weak here. This deflation is justified for this setup. PNCBF's real value is **generalization** (non-convex obstacles, sensor observation, complex/higher-order dynamics, multi-agent) where an analytic CBF cannot be written; the HOCBF is therefore an **oracle/diagnostic standard showing what the learned V_S should have**, not a method to adopt (deploying it would abandon the learning premise) and not a supervision target. Expansion to non-convex obstacles, multi-agent, 3D dynamics, and manipulators is **reserved for discussion with the advisor** and is out of scope for v2.2.0/v2.2.1.

## 4. Root-cause decomposition (stage2_stuck_collision_decomposition)

On the N=2000 deploy run (reproduced 86 stuck / 23 collision / 133 HOCBF-stuck exactly; recomputed CBF fields bit-identical), with the two researcher-inspected obs-distribution 4×4 grids (PNCBF-stuck and HOCBF-stuck; trap-depth proxy = final dist-to-goal, ranges 0.11–7.15 and 0.05–7.10). The grids visually confirm the researcher's observation that **escape is geometrically available yet the agent stucks** — and that HOCBF-stuck cases are pinned in tight clusters exactly at the r+0.05 margin.

### 4.1 Stuck — three fractions
Partitioning the 86 PNCBF-stuck by HOCBF outcome: **HOCBF→goal 40, also-stuck 44, timeout 2.** PNCBF-row escapable 90.7% overall (reproduces the mechanism analysis). **Every PNCBF-stuck episode is single-step escapable under the HOCBF rows (100%)** — the also-stuck cases are not single-step geometrically blocked; HOCBF stucks them via the same oscillatory closed-loop cancellation. **Trap depth does not separate resolved from also-stuck** (final dist 2.33 vs 2.27, clearance-at-stall 0.203 vs 0.201, #obs-within-1m 2.0 vs 2.0 — identical static geometry; what HOCBF resolves is a dynamics/approach difference, not a shallower pocket). The three fractions:

| class | definition | count | frac |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| **filter-fixable** | a better (analytic) CBF resolves to goal, same nominal | 40 | **46.5%** |
| **nominal-fixable** | both myopic CBFs stuck, but single-step-escapable everywhere → needs an obstacle-aware nominal to take the tangential escape | 46 | **53.5%** |
| **planning-hard** | also-stuck AND single-step-blocked → needs look-ahead | 0 | **0.0%** |

**No deployed PNCBF-stuck is single-step myopically blocked.** *Caveat:* single-step escapability is necessary, not sufficient — so 53.5% nominal-fixable is an **upper bound** on what a reactive obstacle-aware nominal could address, and the true planning-hard fraction is ≥ 0 (the single-step test cannot see the oscillatory closed-loop trap). HOCBF's 71 goal→stuck regressions are distinct and worse-conditioned: stall clearance median **0.050** (= the r+0.05 margin), **77.5% single-step blocked** — over-conservatism manufacturing genuinely-blocked margin-stalls where the learned PNCBF threaded through.

### 4.2 Collision — 100% control-authority failure
Attribution of every collision (23 deploy + 283 large-N, in close agreement), with HOCBF as the 0-collision-by-construction reference (Nagumo / comparison-lemma forward invariance under the exact analytic CBF):

| cause | deploy (23) | large-N (283) |
|---|---:|---:|
| **V_S value error** (h ≤ 0, "says safe" at impact) | **0%** | **0%** |
| **learned-invariance violation** (row enforced yet collided) | **0%** | **0%** |
| **discretization** (continuous condition held but dt overshot) | **0%** | **0%** |
| **singular gradient** (‖L_g h‖ < 5e-4) | 21.7% | 22.6% |
| **empty control authority** (α_unsafe braking demand exceeds box-achievable) | 78.3% | 77.4% |

The chain, identical across samples: V_S **alarms early and correctly** (lead 13–17 steps, at a generous clearance ~0.44–0.54 m — *larger* than HOCBF's 0.05 m margin; h → 0.88–0.91 at impact; 0% value error), **but its gradient collapses exactly where braking is needed** (median ‖L_g h‖ 0.08–0.12): with α_unsafe = 100 the row demands A·u ≤ ~−90 while the box achieves only ~−0.2, so the feasible set is **empty at 100% of impacts**; HardNet returns the least-violating max-brake, but a near-degenerate L_g h cannot convert it into effective deceleration. Collisions are **physically avoidable** (0.5 m at 0.7 m/s stops in ~0.12 m) — a **value-function representation/conditioning failure**, not unrecoverable dynamics, not discretization, not too-small a margin.

## 5. Why L_g h collapses (stage2_authority_collapse)

Forward+autograd on stored collision states (reconstruction h / lg_norm max diff 0.0); reference = goal near-obstacle (clearance < 0.6). This **corrects** the §4 wording ("V_S saturates toward the clip h = 1 and the gradient flattens"):

- **NOT the clip/saturation (refuted).** Only **3.4%** of impact states sit at the [−1,1] clip bound; the pre-clip vs post-clip velocity-gradient ratio is median **1.0**. The small gradient is **intrinsic to the learned function**, not a clamp artifact. The clip is a minor (3.4%) two-sided contributor, not the cause.
- **The collapse is structurally small, not vanishing, and universal near the boundary.** ‖L_g h‖ is ~0.40–0.50 for h ≤ 0.75, easing to ~0.20 at h → 1; only 3.4% truly singular. The goal-near and collision curves track each other at matched (h, clearance) — **collisions are where the approach speed makes the universal small gradient *bite*, not where it uniquely degenerates.**
- **Ranked causes:**
  1. **Velocity-sensitivity deficiency (the authority floor).** ‖∂h/∂v‖ median **0.367** vs ‖∂h/∂p‖ **1.419** (ratio **0.26**, ~4× more position-sensitive) and ~5× weaker than the analytic (‖L_g h‖ / ‖2(p−c)‖ = **0.20**), mostly correctly oriented (cosine 0.745, 17.9% misoriented). This is the **48.7% of the danger regime (and ~80% at impact) that no α can fix** — primarily a magnitude problem.
  2. **α_unsafe = 100 grossly over-aggressive (proximate trigger).** Critical-α (largest feasible α given the actual gradient) median **0.08**; the row is infeasible at α=100 for ~97% of h>0 steps; lowering to α_safe=2 would restore feasibility on ~39% of approach steps with the same V_S — *as an open-loop algebraic what-if* (refuted in §6).
  3. NOT clip (refuted); direction secondary (18%); near-boundary-tail under-constraint minor (‖L_g h‖ ~0.15 in the 0.03%-rare clearance < 0.1 tail vs ~0.40 peak).

## 6. α is not the lever (stage2_lower_alpha_deploy)

Testing the proximate trigger directly: a **real closed-loop deploy** sweep of α_unsafe {100, 20, 10, 5, 2, 1, 0.5} on the same N=2000 pool (α_safe fixed at 2; verified pure deploy gain change — α_unsafe enters only as row_upper = −L_f h − α_unsafe·h in the danger regime h > 0).

| α_unsafe | collision | cps |
|---:|---:|---:|
| **100 (baseline)** | **23** | **0.8258** |
| 20 / 10 / 5 | 31 / 34 / 39 | 0.8113 / 0.8190 / 0.8116 |
| 2 / 1 / 0.5 | 52 / 64 / 77 | 0.7976 / 0.7898 / 0.7777 |

**Lowering α_unsafe monotonically INCREASES collisions (23 → 77) and lowers cps; the cps-best α is the baseline 100.** Paired (α=2 vs baseline): of 23 baseline collisions **20 still collide, 3 resolve**, while **32 new collisions appear** (27 goal→collision, 5 stuck→collision) → net 23 → 52. The open-loop critical-α what-if ("39% feasible at α=2") is **refuted** in closed loop: of the baseline collisions 47.8% are authority-bound (critical-α ≤ 0) and only 3/23 of the nominally "alpha-fixable" ones actually resolved.

**Mechanism: feasibility ≠ safety.** At α=100 the infeasible row triggers the least-violating = **maximum-braking** action; lowering α makes the row feasible but *gentle* (ḣ ≤ −α·h, small α), which brakes less and lets the agent approach into more collisions. The aggressive infeasible-max-brake at α=100 is the **most protective the filter can be given the weak L_g h**. **0% of the collision problem is cheaply fixable by α** — this refines authority-collapse's "α is a cheap secondary mitigation" to *not a mitigation at all*. **The entire collision problem falls to the value-function fix.**

## 7. The located fix target (stage2_velocity_dependence_char, stage2_training_velocity_audit)

### 7.1 What the velocity-dependence looks like (stage2_velocity_dependence_char)
Forward+autograd on a constructed grid (goal fixed (3.5,3.5); multi-obstacle layout for contours, single obstacle for the shift/gradient/closing-speed analysis). Velocity-dependence **exists but is ~4–6× too weak and short-clearance-localized**:

- **Boundary shift with velocity** (the qualitative PNCBF signature, present): the learned unsafe boundary extends to **1.11 m approaching** vs **0.68 m receding** (shift **0.438**), but the analytic shifts **1.132 m** (1.68 approach / 0.55 = r_safe collapse receding) → **shift ratio 0.39**. The learned safe set is **position-dominated** — it keeps a large unsafe halo even when receding (0.68 m), whereas the analytic collapses to the surface.
- **Where ∂h/∂v is weak** (the safety-relevant approach-aligned component, by clearance): 0.016, 0.030, 0.257, 0.489, 0.723, **0.883 (peak ~0.5 m)**, …, 0.124 vs analytic 1.0–4.0 — **up to ~60× weaker at the surface, ~5× at clearance 0.2, ~2.3× at 0.5**, and *fine at moderate clearance*. So a velocity-sensitivity loss must be **concentrated on short-clearance fast-approach states, not applied broadly** — the deficiency is local, exactly in the collision regime. (A secondary effect: at extreme near-fast states one ensemble member clips → grads [0.133, 0.0], so the deployed mean halves the gradient to 0.066; minor, since real-data clip rate is 3.4%.)
- **The value's velocity-response itself is weak, not only its gradient**: dh/d(closing speed) is **0.205 at clearance 0.1** (h-change 0.523) vs analytic 1.20 — ~6× too weak, worst near the boundary (ratio 0.17 at clr 0.1 vs 0.27 at 0.6). h *does* rise with closing speed (V_S is not velocity-blind), but far too weakly. **The loss must target h's closing-speed response (and its gradient) at short clearance — targeting gradient sharpness alone would be incomplete.**

### 7.2 Why it is under-realized in code (stage2_training_velocity_audit)
Read-only code audit (live files: `src/frameworks/jt_pncbf/{train.py, losses.py, collection.py}`, `src/frameworks/oc_pncbf/value_target.py` — there is **no** `train_phase5_v4a.py` / `v4c` file; the "v4c" tag refers to the BPTT-through-HardNet **policy** update, `policy_bptt_loss`, not the value loss).

- **The value loss is a single term** (`losses.py:44-66`): `total = lambda_R · mean((prediction − target)²)`, lambda_R = 1.0. Config `lambda_A = 0.0`, `lambda_C = 0.0` exist **only in exp_config and are referenced by no code** — placeholder slots for value terms that do not exist.
- **There is NO CBF-derivative (ḣ + α·h) term anywhere** (verified by grep). **`L_g h = ∂h/∂v` is never directly constrained** — it is shaped *only indirectly* through value (label) MSE. The loss rewards a correct value, not a correct gradient, so a value-correct-but-gradient-weak fit incurs **zero penalty**. This is the **direct structural reason** ∂h/∂v is under-realized. **The fix is "add the missing term" (the empty lambda_C slot), not "strengthen an existing one."**
- **Velocity enters the target only through the dynamics**: target = PNCBF discounted max-over-time avoid of **position-only** signed_h (h_scale 0.35) along a real RK4 rollout (ṗ=v, v̇=u), so same-position-different-velocity states get different targets — structural but **indirect**. obs includes velocity; **no stop-grad** on it (so V_S *can* learn ∂h/∂v; the limitation is purely that the loss does not constrain it).
- **Collection under-samples short-clearance fast-approach**: HardNet-filtered nominal + adaptive Gaussian σ (sigma_min 0.3, sigma_max 2.0, rho_target 0.10) targeting ~10% unsafe episodes — a partial boundary-sampler. But (a) the filter brakes the agent *away* from short clearance — exactly where ∂h/∂v is weakest; (b) sequential trajectories give no same-position-diverse-velocity coverage there; (c) there is no designed near-boundary sampling. This is the **recurring "good policy → no unsafe signal → V collapses" loop**: adaptive σ fixes the unsafe-episode *fraction*, not the same-position-velocity diversity at short clearance that ∂h/∂v learning needs (the near-boundary tail is 0.03% of visited states).
- **No asymmetric hinge** (the v1 failure mode is gone — symmetric MSE to the PNCBF target; no fixed-label classifier either). Only a minor two-sided [−1,1] clamp saturation remains (3.4% of impacts).

**Located cause:** the structurally-intended velocity-dependence is under-realized at short clearance because **(primary) the value loss has no CBF-derivative / gradient term** (∂h/∂v shaped only indirectly by value MSE) and **(contributing) the HardNet-filtered collection under-samples the short-clearance fast-approach states** (with velocity diversity) needed to learn a strong ∂h/∂v there. Both map onto the characterization's target.

## 8. Synthesis and the v2.2.1 direction

**The two deploy-time failures, fully decomposed:**

- **Collision = a learned-value control-authority (forward-invariance) failure.** V_S knows the state is unsafe (0% value error; alarms 13–17 steps early at a generous margin) but `∂h/∂v` is ~5× too weak at short clearance, so the braking demand is infeasible and the agent collides on physically-avoidable approaches. α is not a lever (lowering it worsens safety). The root is `L_g h` magnitude — the one property the analytic CBF keeps (its `L_g ψ1 = 2(p−c)` stays bounded, giving 0 collisions).
- **Stuck = half filter, half nominal, 0% single-step planning-hard.** ~46.5% is resolved by a better (analytic) CBF under the same nominal; ~53.5% needs an obstacle-aware nominal (the goal-straight policy re-enters the oscillatory CBF-cancellation trap, escapable single-step but not closed-loop); the projector is not the cause (L2-QP recovers no progress), and escape is geometrically available in every case.

**Both trace to under-realized learning**: the value's velocity-sensitivity (collision, and plausibly the CBF-side half of stuck — the same weak `∂h/∂v` that cannot manufacture a braking/curving velocity), and the nominal's obstacle-blindness (the nominal-side half of stuck). v2.2.0's contribution is this diagnosis; the fixes are forward work.

**v2.2.1 — strengthen the velocity-dependence (the collision fix).** The located target is **h's closing-speed response (and its gradient) at short clearance / high closing speed**, addressed oracle-free by:
1. **Adding a CBF-derivative auxiliary term** to the value loss (the empty `lambda_C` slot) — penalizing the descent/feasibility condition `ḣ + α·h` so that a weak `∂h/∂v` (which fails the condition) is directly penalized. This is the CBF *definition* (Nagumo), using only known dynamics — **not the analytic `2(p−c)` as a supervision target** (using the oracle as a training signal would defeat the goal of extending to oracle-free, non-convex/sensor settings; the oracle is for diagnosis and evaluation only). Concentrated on short-clearance fast-approach states per §7.1.
2. **Boundary-targeted collection with velocity diversity** — sampling the short-clearance fast-approach states (with same-position-diverse-velocity coverage) that the HardNet-filtered collection currently under-samples.

**A recorded design tension (the central v2.2.1 question).** The current single-term value loss is the most *grounded* choice for CBF learning: a second term creates a trade-off between value-fitting and the derivative condition, and naively adding it tends to lose on at least one (even though a network parameterization that reduces both exists in principle). v1 already experimented with multiple added terms (the asymmetric-hinge family, which failed across 5 attempts for the moving-target reason). So the auxiliary term is justified **only if** the gain from a stronger velocity-dependence outweighs that trade-off. v2.2.1 must therefore: keep the change **single-axis and ablatable** (the term toggleable, its effect on `∂h/∂v` and on cps measured in isolation), **validate small before a full retrain** (confirm the term actually raises `∂h/∂v` at short clearance without degrading value-fitting or re-triggering the saturation/drift failure modes), and respect the documented past failures.

**Out of scope (reserved for advisor discussion).** Expansion to non-convex/polygonal obstacles, multi-agent, 3D dynamics, and manipulators — where the analytic CBF cannot be written and the learned value's generalization is the real payoff — is to be decided after the advisor meeting and is deliberately not pursued in v2.2.0/v2.2.1.
