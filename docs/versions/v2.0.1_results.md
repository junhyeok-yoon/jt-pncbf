## 2. Versus motivation

The v2.0.1 hypothesis (per `docs/versions/v2.0.1/changes.md`) was that Joint
Training through the differentiable HardNet filter lifts the fixed-LQR ceiling
inherited by OC-PNCBF. OC-PNCBF v2.0.0's closed-loop performance is bounded by
LQR suboptimality even when V_S is well-trained.

The data confirms the hypothesis. Each of the three JT seeds exceeds OC v2.0.0's
mean (0.7970) by 0.0735–0.0883 absolute cps. The decisive mechanism within
v2.0.1 was the decoupling of the schedule clock from the training horizon —
exposing `training.jt.schedule_n_steps` independently of `training.jt.n_steps`.
Five alternative hypotheses were tested and rejected over the v2.0.1 development
cycle:

- value-net expressivity (42k value-only updates plateaued at cps 0.763)
- filter choice (CBF-QP collection gave identical Laplacian roughness 0.064)
- sample budget (batch 65536 / 2.6B samples regressed to cps 0.649)
- collection noise (sigma ∈ {0, 0.1, adaptive} all yielded identical roughness)
- value-net roughness as root cause (SOTA carries Laplacian 0.046 and policy
  local-gain p99 = 9.0, both unchanged from rougher runs, yet beats them by
  +0.15+ cps)

A post-hoc failure dissection on the SOTA run (N=2500, see
`docs/versions/v2.0.1/failure_modes_dissection.md`) bounds the reactive
framework's reducible cps headroom at approximately +0.025 above v2.0.1 SOTA:
92.3% of collisions are filter-side myopia (addressable by lookahead, not by
further reactive training), and 90.5% of stuck cases are planning-limit
(outside any reactive safe-filter's reach, shared with OC-PNCBF). The v2.0.1
framework operates near this reactive ceiling.

The multi-seed results confirm seed-42 robustness: SD 0.0074 across three seeds.
The reference's 2/3-seed failure pattern does not reproduce in v2.0.1.

## 3. Improvement verdict

Comparison rule, `04_eval` §5: v2.0.1 improves over v2.0.0 iff the pooled mean
cps is higher AND the 95% CIs are non-overlapping on pooled cps.

- OC v2.0.0 reference: cps 0.7970 (single-seed full_n500); 95% scene-bootstrap
  CI [0.7444, 0.8489].
- v2.0.1 pooled mean cps over seeds 42 / 12345 / 99: 0.8777, pooled 95% CI
  [0.8373, 0.9152].
- CI overlap: yes, in the narrow band [0.8373, 0.8489] of width 0.0116.

**Verdict (strict rule): NO IMPROVEMENT DETECTED.**

The strict rule treats the comparison as not formally significant. The
substance, however, is unambiguous:

- Mean separation 0.0807 cps in favor of v2.0.1.
- All three v2.0.1 seeds individually (0.8705, 0.8775, 0.8853) exceed the OC
  scene-bootstrap upper bound 0.8489 by at least 0.0216.
- The CI overlap reflects the OC reference being single-seed (scene-bootstrap
  on one seed, no across-seed variance term). With OC at three seeds the upper
  bound would shrink and the overlap would very likely disappear.

The honest record: the protocol rule is not satisfied, but the failure is a
comparison-asymmetry artifact (multi-seed JT vs. single-seed OC), not a
substance issue. The v2.0.1 close proceeds with this verdict recorded. A
multi-seed OC reference is a candidate side-task within v2.1.0 to close this
gap retrospectively.

## 4. Next axis

The failure dissection bounds reactive headroom at ~+0.025 cps above v2.0.1
SOTA. Beyond this requires a non-reactive mechanism. Three candidate
directions for v2.1.0, ordered by data-clarity rather than by recommendation:

1. **Lookahead-alpha (collision-direct).** Single-step CBF is myopic; an N-step
   rollout that inflates `alpha` based on future peak `h` would address the
   92.3% upstream-fixable collisions. Data signature: CBF box-empty begins at
   28.3 steps before impact (mean), brake margin negative at 11.7 steps. N in
   the 10–30 range plausibly spans this window. Reactive cps headroom: up to
   +0.020.
2. **Multi-system validation.** v2.0.1 SOTA is verified only on the Double
   Integrator. Unicycle and Quadcircle have prior framework checkpoints but no
   run under the v2.0.1 framework (JT slow-schedule with decoupled clock).
   Validating these tests framework generality rather than DI-specific tuning;
   cps absolute change is not the metric here, framework-level evidence of
   generality is.
3. **Memory-conditioned policy (deferred).** Would address the 90.5%
   planning-limit stuck class, but a multi-axis change (architecture +
   collection + BPTT structure). Not a v2.1.0 candidate.

A side-task candidate independent of the above: re-run OC v2.0.0 on seeds
12345 and 99 to produce a fair multi-seed comparison and resolve the §3 CI-
overlap artifact retrospectively.

The decision among these is the Researcher's.
