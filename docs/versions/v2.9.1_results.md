## 1. The registered axis, and its adjudication

### 1.1 What was registered

`changes.md` declares **one axis: the clearance floors on the TRAINING scene law** — five parameters,
all floors holding a training initial condition away from a surface, with **nothing on the evaluation
path changed**. The motivation, as registered:

- The floors are **binding, not slack**: over 1,433 fresh initial conditions from the shipped
  collector the minimum horizontal obstacle clearance was **0.1026 m** against a floor of **0.10 m**.
- The certificate is **worst exactly where the floors cut**: over 21,505 states stratified by
  clearance, sign disagreement peaks in the **(0.05, 0.10] m** stratum (0.2486 / 0.2922 on the two
  checkpoints measured) and falls monotonically with clearance, while that stratum is **0.75 %** of
  buffer states.
- The eval pool draws `z ~ U(−4, 4)` reaching 0.005 m from the band surface while training cleared it
  by 0.10 m — training never contained the region the value is scored on.

The five floors as set in the run configs: `start_obstacle_clearance` 0.10 → **0.00**,
`goal_obstacle_clearance` 0.10 → **0.05**, `band_margin` 0.10 → **0.00**, `arena_margin` 0.30 →
**0.00**, `init_feasibility_margin` 0.05 → **0.00**.

### 4.2 The OC curriculum collapse — a failed axis with a mechanism

Two OC runs (3D and planar) collapsed. The mechanism was identified **directly on `V`**, not inferred
from infeasibility: the label is
`pncbf_target = lhs + rhs·relu(rhs_full − lhs)`, and as `target_rhs` → 0.9 the label takes its value
from the target net while `relu` admits only upward corrections. The fixed point is
**`V ≡ ceiling ≡ 1.3`** — not 1.0, because the D3 repair moved the ceiling — which empties `{V ≤ 0}`,
after which `alpha_unsafe` 100 makes every row infeasible. At collapse the median `V` had risen
+1.2788 with the maximum pinned at exactly 1.300000 and `frac(V ≤ 0)` = **0.0000**.

**Two guards failed and one worked.** `cps_floor` at −1.5 never fired — the planar collapse bottomed
at −1.1775, above the floor, and ran 505 epochs to term. `L_R` **disarms** at the fixed point, falling
12× below baseline. **`frac(V ≤ 0)` is the working collapse guard** and is what every subsequent
warm-start readout reports.

**The repair:** freeze the schedule (`schedules.gamma_disc.total_epochs` 10000 against a 1000-epoch
run, so `target_rhs` is identically 0.0, `gamma` constant 0.95, `lambda` never leaving warm-up) and
cap the buffer. Verified as a **property**, not an observation.

### 4.5 The `empty_fallback` finding, and two open source defects

`filter.empty_fallback` was believed eval-only. It is not: `make_filter_params` is the single filter
construction path, so the **training collection filter** reads the same key
(`jt_pncbf/collection.py:512`, called from `:103`, `:152`, `:459`). The branch at
`filter_hardnet.py:192` is guarded by `empty_fallback_mode == "kstep"` and `empty_intersection.any()`
and **nothing else** — no training/eval gate.

**Measured, not assumed.** `metrics.csv:collect_infeasible` reads 0.000000 on every run, but that is a
**constant, not a measurement**: every JT run uses `collection.collector: continuing`, whose stats are
built with `mean_projection=0.0, infeasible_fraction=0.0` **hardcoded**
(`jt_pncbf/collection.py:202-204`), and `continuing_collector.py` never references empty, singular or
infeasible. A direct read-only probe (512 scenes × 200 steps, each run's own config and sampler)
found the empty condition **common, not rare**: on L304's checkpoint, EMPTY step-rate **0.049268**,
arising in **64.06 %** of episodes. Two limits stated: measured at the adopted checkpoint's policy and
value rather than integrated over training, and at σ = 0, which makes every figure a **floor**.

**Consequence for the record:** every run of record trains with the fallback resolving to
`{none, k 10}` — the eval-only separation the design intends — and is *scored* under `{kstep, 1, 3}`,
because `v282_agree_gate.py:39` replaces the block wholesale. The three non-3D JT runs that had
briefly carried a per-system sub-block were stopped and relaunched without it.

**Two open source defects, recorded and not fixed** (a source change during the paper cycle was not
approved):

1. The branch at `filter_hardnet.py:192` has **no caller-mode guard**; the docstring at `:187` and the
   config comment at `exp_config.yaml:334` both claim "eval-only", so the property holds **by config
   convention only**.
2. `exp_config.yaml` still ships `quadrotor_planar`, `double_integrator` and `unicycle`
   `empty_fallback` sub-blocks. Because the code does not gate the key to eval, a future run on those
   systems seeding from the shipped config — rather than deriving from a persisted ancestor — would
   train with the k-step branch live.

### 4.7 The planar JT OOM — contention, established with a matched control

`JTPLANAR_V291_R3` died of CUDA OOM at round 768 in `_cat_optional`
(`oc_pncbf/collection.py:830`), needing a 706 MB allocation with **762 MB free** — *above* the 614 MiB
poll floor, which is why polling could not prevent it. It was relaunched with a **zero-key** config
diff, alone on the card, as `JTPLANAR_V291_R4`.

The two runs' `cuda_max_mem_mb` are **identical at 370 of 384 common rounds**, with a contiguous
identical prefix through round 584 and **exact agreement at the fatal round 768 (3076.1 MB)**; 14
rounds in the 586–696 band differ by at most **12.64 MB** (0.5 %) and re-converge. `cps` at **all 51
shared eval rounds** agrees to better than 1e-12. R4 peaked at **3944.8 MB** and finished round 1000
with 7638 MiB free.

So the pair is a matched control differing only in card occupancy, and the OOM is **contention**.
`collection.jt.buffer_cap` is 10⁶ by the deliberate D_V=1M setting and was **not** changed; at 100
episodes per round over 1000 rounds it never approaches binding. An earlier framing —
"uncapped is 3 for 3 on OOM deaths" — **conflated this death with the two OC arm deaths and is
withdrawn**.

A related dead end is recorded: `collection.jt.buffer_capacity` **has no reader**. The JT trainer
reads `collection.jt.buffer_cap` (`jt_pncbf/train.py:605`); `buffer_capacity` is the **OC** key. A
relaunch that set it was stopped ~2 minutes in once the key was found inert.

268** per-episode records on this exact pool. Its margin, 0.0633 m, is 1.08×10⁵ × ε_num, so this was
not a numerical borderline. **The bound was not adjusted** — the failure is the finding.

*Mechanism.* At θ₀ = 166.2°, `cos θ₀ = −0.9712`, so the "envelope" ran full thrust **downward**,
`a(0) = −28.865 m/s²`. A plant at 166° can throttle to zero and get `−g = −9.81`. The plant-favorable
form needs `max(cos θ_min, 0)`; without it `z_up` stops being an upper envelope exactly where the
soundness chain requires `cos θ_min ≥ 0`. **All 15 flags had θ₀ ∈ [116°, 169°]** — every one lived in
that regime.

**Run 2, the corrected form — CERTIFIED, 0 flags.** With `max(·, 0)` restored and everything else
identical, **all four gates hold** and the pool flag count is **0 on both channels**.

| gate | outcome |
|---|---|
| 1 — analytic safe corner | PASS (`z_up_min = 0.0000`) |
| 2 — monotonicity, 200 probes | PASS (0 new flags either perturbation) |
| 3 — no survivable flag, 268 records | PASS **vacuously** — 0 flags to contradict |
| 4 — analytic doomed corner (new) | **PASS, flagged**, margin +0.824687 m |

Gate 4 is what proves non-vacuity, and gate 3 is stated as carrying no evidential weight in this run.
Its arithmetic: at θ₀ = 170° every admissible input gives exactly `z̈ = −g` while θ > 90°; righting
past 90° from ω₀ = 0 takes `t₉₀ = √(2 × 1.396263 / 130.4055) = 0.146336 s`, over which the guaranteed
drop is `1.4(0.146336) + ½(9.81)(0.146336)² = 0.309907 m` against **0.05 m** of clearance.

**Certified statement:** at the 10 s horizon, `fullcb` contains **no scene provably doomed on the
vertical channel**. Closest approaches: floor **−0.0015 m** (scene 269) but that minimum is at
t\* = 0, i.e. it merely *starts* 1.5 mm above the floor; among the 1423 scenes with a genuine dynamic
dip the closest is **−0.01318 m** (scene 1087). Ceiling, restricted to the 1023 scenes with v_z > 0

**What it does not establish:** possibility — a scene not flagged is *not* certified survivable, and

### 4.10 The `fullcb` z-law audit

Stated as the audit states it (`fullcb_z_law.md`). **`fullcb` was not built under the `04_eval` §6.1
attitude-conditioned vertical bound.** The manifest cannot decide the question — `fullcb`,
`evalicz` and `bandfeasible` carry byte-identical `sampler_params`, and no `.invocation.json` exists
— so the code decides it: the §6.1 draw at `scene_init.py:345-388` is gated at `:344` on an
`ic_eval_z` block that is **absent from every shipped and run config**, so control reaches
`scene_init.py:444`, `start_z = rng.uniform(-world_lim, world_lim)`. The payload confirms it
independently: start z ≈ U(−4, +4), mean 0.131, sd 2.274, min −3.999, max 3.995.

Evaluated against the bound the pool would have had, **252 of 2000 scenes (12.6 %) sit outside
`[z_lo, z_hi]`**, and **7 of the 33 freshly drawn top-ups** are outside — the payload's own evidence
that the build sampler carried no band law. Of 23 `quadrotor_3d` pools on disk exactly one carries
the bound, `eval_bandfeasible_quadrotor-3d-d2r_n2000_seed42` (0/2000 outside); all thirteen
`quadrotor_3d` rows L294–L305 and L312 sit on `fullcb`.

**One claim of the audit is corrected here** (full treatment in §10.6): the audit states that *no
ledger row anywhere* sits on the bound-carrying pool. **Twelve do** — ledger lines 152–175, all
v2.7.6 `quadrotor_3d`, citing `bandfeasible sha=185659a7`, which is that pool's digest. They are all
`scoring=legacy`. Nothing else in the audit changes.

**A null the audit reports rather than works around:** per-episode indices for L305's 53 and L312's
36 floor-collision episodes are **not on disk** — the split artifacts persist counts only — so those
two sets cannot be characterised individually. L312's run-local `eval_episodes.csv` was **not**
substituted: it is a different pool and population (77 `band_lower` vs the row's 36).

#### Resolution — `fullcb` stays the pool of record (Researcher decision, 2026-08-14)

The decision is recorded in §13; its grounds belong with the audit and are set down here.

**1. The §6.1 screen is not an impossibility instrument, and the outcome data on this very pool say
so.** The screen flags **252** scenes as outside the vertical bound. L312 ran the full 2000 scenes of
`fullcb` and its own artifact records **51 colliding episodes in total** (§2, integer counts). Even
on the most generous reading — every collision on the row is one of the flagged scenes — **at least
201 of the 252 flagged scenes did not collide** under L312's policy. A screen that flags 252 scenes
of which at least 201 are demonstrably survivable at that operating point is a **conservative
sampling restriction**, not a statement that the flagged scenes are unwinnable. Excluding them would
remove scenes the policy handles.

**2. The one instrument that does certify impossibility finds none.** The corrected constrained-input
doom certificate was run on all 2000 `fullcb` scenes and certifies **0 of 2000** doomed on the
vertical channel — floor and ceiling both — with the ε_num margin and, critically, with **gate 4
passing**: a synthetic θ₀ = 170°, v_z = −1.4, clearance-0.05 state is flagged, so the zero is not
vacuous (§4.9, `fullcb_doom_corrected.md` §3). The closest genuine dynamic approach in the pool is
1.3 cm (scene 1087) and the closest ceiling approach 1.1 cm (scene 833) — near, but not doomed.

**3. What remains outside every instrument on record, stated as a limit and not resolved.**
Obstacle-coupled impossibility is not covered by anything measured in this version: the doom
certificate ignores the obstacle field entirely, and the §6.1 screen is vertical-only. Given that the
`quadrotor_3d` collision decomposition is **53.9 % obstacle on L305 and 29.4 % on L312** (§2.4), this
gap covers a large share of the collisions actually observed. No claim is made that `fullcb` contains
no impossible scene — only that no instrument on record identifies one.

**Consequence.** `fullcb` remains the `quadrotor_3d` pool of record; **the thirteen `quadrotor_3d`
rows are not re-scored**. This is a decision, not a measurement: it rests on grounds 1 and 2 while
ground 3 stays open, and it is reversible if an obstacle-aware instrument later identifies scenes on
this pool that no admissible input can survive.
