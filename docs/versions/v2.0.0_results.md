## 2. Implementation

### 2.1 Infrastructure (`src/common/`, `src/envs/`, `src/eval/`)

Built in dependency order, each step independently verified, so that the six-test
verification harness (`05_code` §5) is green at step 14:

1. **scene_init** (train + eval) — scene tensor generation.
2. **System abstraction + per-system dynamics** — `Protocol`-based System interface;
   differentiable, batched `f(x)`, `g(x)` for DI and Unicycle.
3. **RK4 integrator** — framework-agnostic batched single step taking `System.dynamics`.
4. **signed-h + outcome resolver** — state + scene to ground-truth `h`; trajectory + scene
   to outcome under the `01_env` §1.6 priority `collision -> goal -> oob -> stuck -> timeout`.
5. **observation function** — state + scene to observation; DI world-relative, Unicycle
   body-frame, both Top-K. Prerequisite of steps 9-12, hence placed ahead of them.
6. **LQR nominal policy** — per-system analytic gain, including the Unicycle virtual-state
   mapping.
7. **batched rollout utility** — `(policy, filter, scene, x_0)` to trajectory; shared by
   plotting and the pool builder.
8. **trajectory plot module** — 4x4 grid renderer with three-trajectory overlay.
9. **value network** — MLP + head + ensemble.
10. **control network** — MLP + softsign/tanh head, pre-activation `z` exposed.
11. **CBF-QP filter** (proxsuite wrapper) — autograd `L_f h`, `L_g h`.
12. **HardNet projection filter** — closed-form, box-aware, with BPTT detach.
13. **bootstrap routine** — within-seed and across-seed CI.
14. **pool builder** — `data/secured_data/pools/` with SHA-256 reproducibility; finishes
    before any train/eval script because pools are immutable first-commit artifacts
    (`06_workflow` §6.3) and a run's metrics are only meaningful against the exact pool.

Filters (11-12) require the value network (9) for their autograd paths.

### 2.2 OC-PNCBF framework (`src/frameworks/oc_pncbf/`)

Five dependency-ordered components on the step-14 foundation:

- **O1 — generic filtered rollout.** `(system, policy, filter, scene, x_0, max_steps)` to a
  trajectory plus per-step intervention mask and infeasibility flag; framework-agnostic
  (JT reuses it).
- **O2 — value-target module.** PNCBF backward avoid recurrence (`03_train` §2.1.1) and
  RPCBF soft-max (§2.1.2) as a pure function, with target_rhs mixing and the gamma schedule.
- **O3 — collection + weak-supervision labeling.** Fixed-LQR collection, single buffer (no
  action-noise schedule, no two-buffer machinery), D_A/D_C anchors, with an unsafe-signal
  check guarding against V -> 0 collapse.
- **O4 — trainer** (`train.py`). Value loss (reach regression + D_A/D_C hinges), AdamW,
  grad-clip, Polyak target, smoke stage; writes TensorBoard / metrics.csv / checkpoints /
  status.json.
- **O5 — eval + full training.** Final-eval runner and in-loop eval wired into the trainer;
  learned `V_S` fed to the CBF-QP via `make_h_fn`.

### 2.3 Trainer / eval components

- CUDA/FP32 batched path: collection, replay tensors, minibatch assembly, and evaluation
  (`05_code` §7).
- Replay as a sliced FIFO buffer.
- In-loop eval (N=200) selects best.pt by cps; full eval (N=500) reports; the two pools are
  disjoint, separating selection from reporting.
- OC final plots overlay LQR-only and LQR + CBF-QP per panel.
- Online insertion appends one extra active obstacle slot rather than replacing one.
- OC halt set: NaN/Inf loss, cps floor, early-stop patience (no policy-gradient-leak or
  sigma halts, since OC has no learned policy or action-noise schedule).

### 2.4 Scene sampler draw order

Start, goal, obstacle count/radii, corridor flag, centers, initial velocity — start and
goal first because corridor centers are defined relative to the start-goal segment.

## 3. Development trajectory

### 3.1 First working baseline

First full randomized run reached in-loop cps 0.722 / collision 0.080 at step 16000; the
first O5 filter-works check passed (7 of 32 plotted scenes: LQR-only collides, LQR + CBF-QP
avoids). After the five-outcome resolver and the metric
`cps = reach - 2*collision - stuck - 0.5*(oob + timeout) - 0.3*infeasibility` were adopted,
the pre-alignment checkpoint `v2.0.0__20260528-145754__seed42` (step 22000) scored full-pool
cps 0.654 / reach 0.896 / collision 0.080 / saturation 0.482. This is the baseline all later
changes are compared against.

### 3.2 Fixed-obstacle control

A single-centered-obstacle run (`v2.0.0__20260528-192106__seed42`, dedicated fixed pools)
reached full-pool collision 0.000 at reach 0.994. This established that the value/CBF stack
is capable of collision-free evaluation and that the residual randomized-distribution gap is
distribution-driven, not a stack limitation.

### 3.3 Dead end — distance-saturated observation

Distance-saturating Top-K features (`obs_d_cut = 2.5`) regressed the retrain
(`v2.0.0__20260528-203610__seed42`) to cps 0.456 / collision 0.148; an alpha sweep never
brought collision under 0.136. Mechanism: saturation attenuates the distant-obstacle signal
the PNCBF backward avoid recurrence needs to anticipate collisions early. Reverted; raw Top-K
restored; baseline recovery confirmed exactly (cps 0.654, collision 0.080). The reference uses
raw Top-K with no cutoff by design.

### 3.4 Dead end — gamma-schedule change

Changing the discount to a long-horizon anchor (init 0.975, final 0.9975), and passing the
scheduled value directly into the recurrence without the `gamma = exp(-lambda*dt)` conversion,
regressed the retrain (`v2.0.0__20260528-224506__seed42`) to cps 0.542 / collision 0.118. The
change had been made without first reading the reference's actual schedule structure; the
correct horizon-anchored lambda schedule was only established by the later fact-finding.

### 3.5 Dead end — collection truncation

Truncating collected LQR trajectories at the first terminal event (so post-event states do not
enter the value buffer or the avoid recurrence) regressed the run
(`v2.0.0__20260528-143809__seed42`) to peak cps 0.345 / collision 0.215, versus the
pre-truncation peak cps 0.722 / collision 0.080. The full-horizon avoid recurrence needs the
post-event tail to propagate the avoid value backward. Reverted; the clean post-revert run
(`v2.0.0__20260528-145754__seed42`) recovered to in-loop cps 0.708 at step 22000.

### 3.6 Reference fact-finding

A pass against `unified_oc_pncbf` documented the reference value target, schedules, network,
filter, dynamics, and collection with file:line provenance, and exposed that the baseline had
diverged from the reference on ~14 independent axes at once.

### 3.7 Reference-aligned rebuild

All ~14 axes were re-aligned in a single rebuild (permitted as reproduction, not novelty
stacking). Smoke confirmed wiring (schedule prints, raw-vs-clipped value head, active velocity
clamp; 31 tests passed). The full run `v2.0.0__20260529-001441__seed42` was configured for
500k value steps and terminated at 100k once converged. In-loop peaked at step 40000;
collision reached 0.005 by 40k and held through 100k. Full-pool final on best.pt (step 40000)
gives the §1 numbers.

### 3.8 R ablation

Single-axis ablation on DI LQR `R` (0.1 / 1.0 / 5.0, all else fixed, each to 40k) isolated its
contribution (§5).

### 4.2 Intentional deviations from the reference (kept)

- dt = 0.05 (reference 0.1); the horizon-anchored schedule divides by dt, so gamma is correct
  at our dt automatically.
- v_init uniform[0, 0.5] (reference 0); broadens the velocity distribution `V_S` learns.
  Unablated.
- cps + five-outcome / stuck metric (reference uses a simpler composite without stuck);
  only the collision component is compared directly to the reference.
- LQR nominal with R = 1.0 (reference uses PD); LQR kept with softened gain.

## 5. Causal analysis

| symptom (improvement) | cause | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| saturation 0.48 -> 0.15 | nominal LQR `R` | sat 0.39 / 0.16 / 0.03 across R = 0.1 / 1.0 / 5.0, collision invariant 0.005 |
| collision 0.08 -> 0.004 | training budget / V_S quality | R-invariant; ~30x larger budget is the salient change |
| contour smoothing | budget + value head | contour shape similar across all three R |
| (no contribution) | gamma schedule | best at step 40k, pre-ramp; ramp slightly hurt cps after |

- **Saturation <- nominal R.** At step 40000, saturation was 0.393 / 0.160 / 0.028 for
  R = 0.1 / 1.0 / 5.0, monotone in R, collision 0.005 throughout. A small R yields a high-gain
  LQR whose nominal action rides the bound (bang-bang); saturation_rate is exactly the
  bound-pinned fraction. Confirmed in the control plots: R = 0.1 reintroduces chattering,
  R = 1.0 is far less pinned.
- **Collision <- budget / V_S quality.** Collision did not move with R (0.005 across a 50x
  range), so it is not a nominal effect; the ~30x training increase (epochs, grad steps,
  collect size, 64x batch) is what accompanies the drop to 0.004. In-loop collision fell to
  0.005 by step 40000 and held.
- **Contour smoothing <- budget + value head.** Contours were similar across R, so the
  less-fragmented adopted contour is not an R effect. A tanh head saturates near +-1 and
  drives the spatial gradient of `h` toward zero on the safe side, flattening the contour and
  starving the CBF-QP; the raw-linear-with-readout-clip head keeps the gradient alive inside
  the clip range.
- **gamma schedule was not a driver.** Best is at step 40000, inside warmup where gamma is
  pinned near its initial value and target_rhs is 0. After the ramp engaged past step 60000,
  cps drifted down (0.823 -> 0.79) through reach loss and rising stuck — mildly harmful, not
  the cause of the improvement.
- **R = 1.0 is the cps optimum.** R = 5.0 over-conservative (reach 0.875, stuck 0.110, cps
  0.700); R = 0.1 high saturation (0.393) and elevated stuck (0.090); R = 1.0 the sweet spot
  (cps 0.823 at step 40000). Stuck is U-shaped in R, lowest at R = 1.0.

## 7. Remaining weaknesses

- Infeasibility 0.193 > reference 0.126, unchanged from the prior baseline (0.206); collision
  is near zero, so the filter stays safe but with the slack relaxation active more often than
  the reference.
- Stuck 0.062 (6% barely-moving episodes), likely part of the cost of the conservatism that
  pushes collision to 0.004.
- Training budget / schedule timing unoptimized: best at step 40000 of 500000, schedule ramp
  mildly harmful past 40k. Early-stop reintroduction and a single-axis schedule study deferred.
- Single seed (42); CIs are scene-bootstrap, not across-seed.
- Two kept deviations unablated: v_init randomization and LQR-with-R=1.0 over the reference PD.

## 8. Lessons

- Reproduce the reference before innovating; a baseline drifted on ~14 axes made every symptom
  unattributable and was the largest time cost.
- Verify current config/protocol and the reference before proposing a change, and frame it as
  current X -> proposed Y, reason Z (the two gamma regressions came from skipping this).
- Single-axis ablation isolates causation (the R ablation); reproduction may be one multi-axis
  step, attribution may not.
- Heuristics: saturation -> suspect the nominal first; collision/contour -> suspect training
  budget first; a schedule you are sure matters may not be the driver — check where best falls
  relative to it.
- Negative results are cheap when the data catches them early: observation-saturation, gamma,
  and truncation were each caught in one retrain and reverted with exact baseline recovery.

## 9. Closure and handoff

OC-PNCBF DI is closed at the adopted baseline. JT inherits the value/CBF stack (value network
and ensemble convention, CBF-QP with autograd `L_f h` / `L_g h`, `make_h_fn` adapter), the
reference-aligned configuration, the shared verification infrastructure (generic filtered
rollout O1, eval core, pools, bootstrap CI), and the open weaknesses (infeasibility 0.193,
stuck 0.062, unoptimized budget/schedule) as starting points. Deferred and out of scope here:
multi-seed campaigns, the schedule-timing single-axis study, early-stop reintroduction, and
ablation of the two kept deviations.
