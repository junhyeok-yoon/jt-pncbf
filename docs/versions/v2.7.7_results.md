## 2. Versus motivation

**The version has no `docs/versions/v2.7.7/changes.md` and no registered prediction.** The
directory did not exist before the close retrieve. `06_workflow` §2.1 requires `changes.md` on
disk before code changes begin, with the Executor's first action being to read it; the sole

`data/previous_runs/v2.7.7/phase_v277_deck_report.md:3-5`. There is therefore no hypothesis to
score, and nothing here can be a registered-prediction outcome. That is a governance fact, not
a data fact, and it is the first item of §4.

Against the milestone contract, which is what this version can be scored on:

- **Asset contract met.** 25 of 27 milestones PASS, 2 authorized skips, every asset maps to a
  named script under `scripts/deck/`, and exactly one file survives per figure.
- **One target missed and correctly not forced.** M2's thrust-channel share was targeted at
  comparator 0.0158 overall / 0.074 at the band boundary and ours 0.20357 / 0.71 — the
  band-feasible pool figures quoted in `docs/index.md:12`. The canonical recompute returns
  comparator 0.0727 / 0.0717 and ours 0.3229 / 0.7693
  (`deck_assets/authority_split_canonical.json`). The artifact values were printed and the gap
  flagged. §5 states what follows, because the discrepancy is larger than it looks.
- **Zero-forced-number discipline held.** Every cross-check in §1 is an assert inside the
  generating script, and the one mismatch was reported rather than reconciled.

**The two findings that were not commissioned are the version's real output.** Neither is an
asset.

**M23 / M24 — the analytic feasibility flag is not a valid lower bound, and the route is
closed for now.** The M21 analytic `dz_min` flags 105 of 2000 ICs as band-infeasible, with
recall 0.73 and precision 0.70 against an eval-only rollout. Audited against the deployed
configuration (M23) its precision falls to 0.58, with 34 of 44 false positives clear misses,
not near misses. The simulator-consistent replacement (M24) reaches recall 1.00 but precision
0.328, flagging 195 ICs with 131 false positives. Both were stopped without revision and the
next term was named — control suboptimality and thrust-torque coupling, an OCP. Consequence:
the feasibility-corrected headline that this route would have produced (cps 0.8051 → 0.9032
analytic, or 0.9103 simulator-consistent) **is not usable**, and is left bracketed. A route that
would have raised the headline by 0.10 was closed by its own audit.

**M27 supplies the scoring the paper needs, and M25 is superseded by it.** The with-z-limit
0.8051 charges the policy for crossings it had no authority to prevent: all 45 crossings above
the holding cone were tilted beyond 60 degrees for the entire run before crossing, so the
initial condition, not the policy, put them below the floor, and 43 of the 45 still reach the
goal. The without-z-limit 0.9078 charges nothing at all. M27 charges exactly the 19 crossings
that occurred inside the cone. 0.8793 sits between the two bounds and the sanity check passes.

## 5. The M2 discrepancy, and what it changes

This is the one item in this version that propagates outside it.

The thrust-channel authority share of the comparator is **0.0158 on the band-feasible pool** and
**0.0727 on the canonical pool** — a factor of 4.6. The corresponding shares for the deployed
system are 0.20357 and 0.3229. `docs/index.md:12` quotes the band-feasible pair without naming
the pool, and the same pair has been used in prose as "the thrust channel carries under
2 percent of the certificate's authority". On the canonical pool it carries 7.3 percent, so the
qualitative statement — the authority is torque-dominant, and the vertical extension moves a
large share of it onto thrust — survives, but the specific figure does not.

Consequences, all of which are cheap to fix and expensive to leave:

- Any slide, abstract, or paragraph quoting 0.0158 or "98.4 percent torque" must either name the
  band-feasible pool or be restated with 0.0727 on the canonical pool. Mixing them is a pool mix.
- The canonical pool is the one every other headline number in this lineage uses. Unless there is
  a reason to prefer band-feasible here, the canonical pair should become the quoted one.
- The gap itself is informative and unexplained: restricting to band-feasible ICs reduces the
  comparator's measured thrust share by 4.6 times. No record explains why. It is not adjudicated
  here.
