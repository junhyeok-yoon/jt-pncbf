## Part II — Exp 1: loss channel (control-deficit penalty)

Definition. Per BPTT step, delta_u_t = P_CBF(u_nom_t) - P_boxCBF(u_nom_t), where P_CBF is
the box-free half-space projection (the raw control the CBF asks for) and P_boxCBF is the
deployed box-aware output. delta_u = 0 iff the box does not bind the safety correction;
large at empty intersection (b carries alpha_unsafe = 100 on h > 0). Loss:
L_deficit = w_d * mean_t[ 1{nonsingular} * penalty(||delta_u_t||) ].

Dose-response (sq_cap penalty, singular-masked, cap = 4.0), seed 42, n2000 re-selected:

| arm | cps [95% CI] | reach | stuck | collision | saturation | empty-intersection infeas | mean_speed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| v2.3.0 baseline | 0.8698 [0.8527, 0.8869] | 0.952 | 0.025 | 0.008 | ~0.137 | 0.0355 | 1.162 |
| w_cal (w_d=1) | 0.8109 | — | — | — | ~0.14 | 0.0387 | 1.115 |
| D1 (w_d=10) | 0.5843 | — | — | — | ~0.12 | 0.0402 | 1.046 |

(baseline empty-intersection / singular split 0.0355 / 0.1188 is carried forward from the
v2.4.0 attribution series, not a freshly recomputed JSON on disk — see Disposition.)

Two structural defects, both established with numbers:

1. sq_cap is inert on-target. The cap makes ||delta_u_used||^2 = cap^2 constant for
   ||delta_u|| > cap, so its gradient is exactly zero on capped steps — which are the
   high-alpha_unsafe empty-intersection states the axis targets (clip_frac 0.22-0.31). The
   observed target MOVEMENT is the wrong sign (empty-intersection UP 0.0355 -> 0.0387 ->
   0.0402) and is entirely a conservatism side effect: mean_speed falls monotonically
   (1.162 -> 1.115 -> 1.046) and cps collapses. sat_task = 0 across all arms confirms the
   saturation metric is purely filter-box-binding (the policy's softsign output never
   reaches the bound within tolerance), so the penalty only ever acted through
   slowing-down, not through better authority management.
2. The fix (Huber) is grad-tail-blocked. Replacing the squared cap with a Huber penalty
   restores a nonzero, bounded gradient above the cap — but that on-target gradient flows
   through the projection's state-dependent coefficients (h-Hessian, the alpha 2->100 jump,
   box-argmin geometry), the exact explosion path root-caused in v2.4.0 Step 4. The
   grad-tail gate FAILED at every dose {1, 3, 10, 30}, with per-sample max 131x the OFF
   T=30 reference at w_d = 1. "On-target gradient" and "gradient stability" are mutually
   exclusive on the empty-intersection state set under the loss channel.

Dose ceiling. w_d > 10 (sq_cap) is itself tail-limited (per-sample p99 8.58x at 30x, 28.9x
at 100x of the OFF reference), so the admissible sq_cap range is capped at 10 independently
of the Huber issue.

## Part III — Exp 2: observation channel (delta_u_{t-1} as policy input)

Thesis. Feed the deficit as state instead of loss: obs_aug_t = [obs_t (dim 19),
delta_u_{t-1}] -> dim 21, delta_u_{-1} = 0. The learning signal then reaches the policy
through the ordinary task-return BPTT gradient (obs -> action -> future cost), and the
deficit feature is detached, so the coefficient Jacobian is not traversed. The real
question Exp 1 left open: given the deficit as state, can the policy AVOID box-binding by
anticipating/steering rather than only by slowing?

   grad_norm_pi max was 2011 over the run (vs 352,599 for the sq_cap w_cal arm). The

2. Target UNMOVED. Empty-intersection infeasibility 0.0429 vs baseline 0.0355 — not reduced

   gross slowing — it simply did not reduce box-binding.

Verdict: CONSERVATISM ECHO / target-refuted. The observation channel removes the loss
channel's gradient obstacle but does not change the behavioral outcome on the target metric.

## Part IV — Mechanism finding (the yield)

Two channels with disjoint gradient paths — a loss penalty (coefficient-Jacobian path) and
a detached observation (task-return path) — produce the same on-target result: the
empty-intersection infeasibility component does not decrease. Combined with sat_task = 0 and
the monotone mean_speed decline under the loss channel, this identifies the obstacle as
behavioral rather than informational: in this double-integrator setting the only policy
action that reduces box-binding is to approach slower, and cps prices stuck (-1) far above
the empty-intersection infeasibility share (~0.3 x 0.036 ~ 0.011), so there is no net policy
gain available on this component. Empty-intersection infeasibility and liveness are two ends
of one trade-off here. The remaining lever on this component is filter-side — a second-order
CBF condition on the learned V (an HOCBF-style Vdot-chain, a 02_control-level mechanism) —
not any policy-side reshaping.
