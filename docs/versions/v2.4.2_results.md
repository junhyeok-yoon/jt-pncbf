at <= ~0.47 (fixed-point amplification 1/(1−beta) <= 1.9× vs Exp 1's 6.1×). New telemetry:

## Part III — Mechanism findings (the scientific yield)

1. **Tail-bootstrap ratchet — confirmed, conservative direction.** The label operator is the
   mixed estimator M_c(W) = lhs + c·relu(rhs(W) − lhs); its tail is evaluated at unfiltered
   re-roll endpoints that are structurally off-support (speed ≈ 2.07 ≈ v_max; 13% post-collision;
   never supervised — realization R1/R3), the one-sided relu passes the endpoint elevation upward
   only, and the schedule ramps the amplification. Measured: tail push 0.045 → 0.298 (Exp 1);
   counterfactual 2×2 attribution: schedule ×4.67, V-elevation ×1.37 (`tail_cross.json`).
   Formalized as theory note Rev. 2 Thm "mixed estimator" (contraction beta = c·gamma^T;
   truncation channel downward; tail channel upward-only ⇒ failure mode is over-flagging, never
   optimism) — matching the observed signature (low collision, rising infeasibility, reach decay).
2. **The ceiling is not in the bias channels.** Halving the ratchet (Exp 2) halved every targeted
   mechanism metric yet left the re-selected cps unchanged (0.7466 ≈ 0.7448, ~0.12 below
   baseline). Attribution of the ceiling: conditioning-authority loss (below), not label bias.
3. **Authority loss / interior flatness.** The unfiltered pi_b is a hazard-blind charger; by the
   authority theorem (Rev. 2 §4: ||∇V^T g|| <= L_h · input-to-peak sensitivity; interior flatness
   where the trajectory attains the clip ceiling) its certificate carries no filter-steering
   gradient where recovery is demanded. Correlates: singular infeasibility 0.089–0.122 at the
   bests; residual stuck 0.90–0.95 corridor/filter-active; endpoint statistics (R3).
4. **Why `task_stored` is immune at the same schedule:** its tail rides the ON-support stored
   trajectory (small tail error e); curve-shape deltas +0.004/+0.033 vs +0.259/+0.195.
5. **n_vs=2 min-ensemble is insufficient off-support:** the tail already used the min head
   (losses.py:128) and still elevated (V_target(x30) −0.451 → −0.332).
6. **Gamma-floor / reselection validity.** Exp 1's best sits at k_dagger = 22.2 < T_stop = 25
   (the discount-blind braking range; Rev. 2 Cor "reselection validity"); the empirical
   collision contrast (0.065 vs 0.033) is stage-confounded in this run (realization R2) — the
   floor H_0 >= 37 remains a registered follow-up, deliberately not co-changed.
7. **Sigma's dual role (margin + authority).** corr(sigma, rho_unsafe_label) = +0.62,
   collect_infeasible 0.16 → 0.32: the adaptive-noise margin of the baseline is also its
   gradient-mollification channel (Rev. 2 Rem "noise as a mollifier") — `task_raw_lagged` removed
   both at once. [mechanism grade]
8. **Conditioning-independent eps0 base.** The brake-comparator probe puts v2.3.0 / v2.4.1 /
   v2.4.2-early at +0.206 / +0.215 / +0.176 (R5): a ~0.2 estimation-error floor exists across
   conditioning schemes; only the late excess (0.371) is raw_lagged-specific. The estimator, not
   the conditioning operator, bounds eps0.
9. **Falsifier verification (O6 outcome).** The band-localization prediction's antecedent (small
   eps0 at tail states) was violated, and the instruments detected exactly that (band spread,
   eps0 growth): a verification of the runtime falsifier, not of the bound — recorded in Rev. 2.

Closure statement: with `task_stored`(+sigma), `brake` (S1), `learned_recovery` (S2), and
`task_raw_lagged` (Exp 1/2) all measured, the self-conditioning family has populated all three
vertices of the soundness/informativeness/competence trilemma (Rev. 2 Rem "trilemma"); **the
conditioning axis is closed**. The proposed next axis (MC-PNCBF Stage A: analytic maneuver-family
