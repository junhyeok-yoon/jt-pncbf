## 2. Versus motivation

**Encoder (`prop:obs-chain`) — mechanism confirmed, deployment value refuted by the same
measurement.** The registered prediction was that the crossing ‖Δu‖ ratio falls from ~33× toward
~1. The D3 protocol was reproduced faithfully (active-step truncation via
`first_physical_event_step`, within-episode ‖Δu‖, crossing = hard top-k order change, terminal
0.48 for both encoders so the encoder is the only difference); the hard reproduction matches the
recorded baselines at both rates, which validates the instrument.

| rate | hard (cross / non) | soft β=30 (cross / non) | ratio hard → soft |
|---|---|---|---|
| 500 Hz | 32.27× (0.6191 / 0.0192) | **1.54×** (0.0304 / 0.0198) | 20.98× reduction |
| 20 Hz (deployment) | 1.84× (1.3672 / 0.7418) | **2.34×** (1.8918 / 0.8086) | 0.79× — no gain |

The prediction is **met at 500 Hz** and **not met at 20 Hz**. The two readings are one fact: the
argsort discontinuity is measured against per-step state motion, and at 20 Hz that motion
(non-crossing ‖Δu‖ 0.742) already swamps the crossing jump — hard is only 1.84× there, so there
was almost nothing to repair. At 500 Hz the non-crossing baseline falls to 0.019 and the encoder
jump stands alone. **`prop:obs-chain` is therefore rate-conditional**: the discontinuity it
identifies is real, and its practical magnitude is a function of dt. This is the version's
principal finding and belongs in the theory document as a remark; it also discharges part of the
precondition already registered for the deferred dt-randomization experiment.

Observation continuity itself passed by construction: G1 bit-wise parity at the hard limit across
the tie battery, permutation invariance, and continuity through a k-th-position crossing.

**Terminal (ω_G 0.30, w_settle_ang 1.0 derived).** T2 failed at 0.8620. Terminal and encoder were
registered as non-separable within Stage 1 (changes.md header), so the failure is not attributed
here; the JT-phase separation (re-scoring the deliverable under ω_G=0.48) was not run and remains
the open measurement.

**Encoder cost is attributable and is not a terminal artifact.** Terminal-confound re-scoring at
the value phase (`v281_confound_rescoring.py`, canonical n2000, band-terminate): the hard v2.7.6
value `…043415` under the **harder** new terminal scores cps 0.4761 / reach 0.8185 / collision
0.1120, while the β=30 soft value under the **easier** old terminal scores cps 0.1608 / reach
0.6910 / collision 0.1930. The soft encoder is worse on every axis even with the terminal
advantage reversed, so the collision gap is the encoder's.

**Two hypothesised failure modes were refuted, and the surviving one was located.** V2: authority
is healthy — ‖L_g V̂‖ median 0.674 soft vs 0.534 hard, singular share 8.9% vs 10.6%. V4:
far-obstacle blindness is 0.0 for both; every collision had its nearest cylinder well inside
d_c=3.0 m ten steps before contact. V3: the open-space filaments do not align with the σ-knot
circles. What remains is **mixing dilution** — near the h*=0 boundary the soft certificate's
‖∇ₓV̂‖ median is 1.58 against hard's 5.84, i.e. the gradient is redistributed away from the
boundary into open space, which is what the contour shows as offset crescents and filaments.

**β was screened, and the screen changed the registered constant.** changes.md §4 fixes
β = 1/(0.5 m) = 2.0 by a dimensional tie to the smoothstep width, not by measurement. The screen
over {2, 6, 12, 30, 50} moved OC collision 0.186 → 0.154 → 0.106 → ~0.09 (flat to 50) and cps
0.216 → 0.635, with the crossing ‖Δobs‖ ratio staying ≤ 66 against hard's 211 throughout;
saturation begins at β = 30, which was adopted. The screen's registered collision threshold
(≤ 0.06) was calibrated against a comparator measured under a different terminal and was void as
registered — a Strategist calibration error, recorded rather than re-fitted.

**The screen also uncovered a defect in the encoder implementation.** The iterated soft-rank
formed its residual by subtraction (`1 − Σ w`), which cancels in float32 as the top slot's mass
approaches 1. At β ≥ 6 this collapsed the normaliser to ~1e−8, producing a finite but ~1e24
position-gradient; dotted with velocity in the drift it gave L_f h ≈ −6.7e21 and numerically
defeated the CBF-QP, which reported primal-infeasible on a fully finite datum — impossible
geometrically, since the QP's slack is bounded only below. At β = 50 the residual rounded to
exactly zero and slots 1–4 lost their gradients silently. The repair derives slot occupancy from
the active mask (discrete, hence no position-gradient path) and carries cross-slot exclusion as a
masked logsumexp complement, so no subtraction of the dominant term occurs. G1 had not caught it
because the β→∞ case delegates to `top_k_obstacles` and the tests ran in float64 — the limit gate
never exercised the soft path.

## 3. Improvement verdict

**No improvement, and the negative result is the yield.** The deliverable regressed against the
v2.8.0 reference on both axes it could be read on (gate cps 0.6496 vs 0.7919; gate collision
0.0660 vs 0.0440), and T2 failed. But the version answers the question it was opened to ask: the
observation discontinuity that `prop:obs-chain` identifies is real, is removable, and is
**invisible at the deployment rate**. That is measured, not argued, on a validated instrument.
The soft-rank axis is retired on this basis rather than on the T2 failure — raising β further
converges to the hard encoder, which the screen's saturation already shows.

Two by-products stand independently of the verdict: the D3 rate sweep is a partial discharge of
the dt-randomization precondition, and the failure localised the correct repair for the next
version (§4).

## 4. Next axis

v2.8.2 (Researcher-decided): revert to the v2.8.0 `hard_topk` encoder, preserving the `soft_topk`
implementation and its gates in the tree; carry the infeasibility work forward as the version's
subject. Registered budget economy: OC 30000 and JT 30000 steps, with batch size and per-step
training volume reduced for iteration speed — v2.8.1's JT in-loop cps was 0.5400 at 30000 against
0.5443 at the 36000 best and 0.4671 at 50000, so the second half of the budget bought nothing.
Infeasibility is judged on early-trajectory behaviour: if it is not visibly controlled early, it
does not recover later. Ledger rows carry `n_steps` explicitly, since v2.8.0's rows are 50k and
are not step-matched.

Deferred, unchanged: dt randomization (gated on the disturbance rate sweep, now partly informed by
this version's D3 result); encoder relabel weighting (B-scheme); conditional-clearance pool via
κ-arrest rejection sampling. Newly deferred from this version: an **exact min-surface-distance
observation channel** — the hard encoder's discontinuity lives in the slot assignment, not in the
distance, and min_i(‖p−c_i‖ − r_i) is continuous through a rank crossing while being exactly the
quantity h_star depends on; it changes obs_dim, so it is a single-axis version of its own, not a
mid-version edit.
