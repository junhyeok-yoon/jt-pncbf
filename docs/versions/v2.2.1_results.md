# v2.2.1 — Results: CBF-derivative L_feas (∂h/∂v strengthening) + h-band activation

Verdicts for the v2.2.1 axis. Facts and full traces live in the build-log
(`docs/versions/v2.2.1/cbf_deriv_feasibility_build.md`) and the two diagnosis docs; this records what
the axis established and why it closes. SOTA (v2.0.1, cps 0.8852, collision 0.006) UNCHANGED.

## What v2.2.1 tested

Hypothesis (from v2.2.0): deploy collisions are a control-authority failure — deployed ‖L_g h‖ =
‖∂h/∂v‖ is ~5× weaker than the analytic 2(p−c) (median 0.20 vs 1.79). v2.2.1 added an oracle-free
box-feasibility auxiliary value-loss L_feas that pressures the recoverable-boundary descent condition
m(x) = L_f h + α(h)·h − u_max·‖L_g h‖₁ ≤ −γ, the only escape from which is to grow ‖L_g h‖. Three runs
across two sub-axes: (1) fixed weight (0.1 @ 10k seed-0; 0.3 @ 42k seed-0), (2) the h-band activation
change (γ 0.05→0.3 + deployed-h band [−0.3, 0.1], weight 0.1, seed-42, SOTA budget).

## Verdict 1 — the mechanism works; the fixed scalar weight does not

L_feas lifts deployed ‖L_g h‖ on the recoverable boundary with no V-collapse and no MSE degradation.
In the seed-42 h-band run, gate lg_norm rose from ~0.20 to a sustained 0.55–0.65 (peak 0.74 @ 26.6k),
crossing 0.48 at ~step 11k and exceeding all prior runs; probe_h_mean stayed strongly negative
(−0.90→−0.51, never toward 0), L_R bounded (~0.05 post-warmup), L_feas_weighted ≈ 2% of L_R (a gentle
nudge throughout). weight=0 parity is exact (max |param diff| = 0.0), so the SOTA-reproduction path is
bit-unchanged.

But raising the fixed weight is NOT the lever. Weight 0.3 @ 42k ended at lg_norm ~0.34 — BELOW the
weight-0.1 @ 10k run's 0.48 — because the term's magnitude is set by α(h)·h and the training schedule,
not the scalar weight: on a trained net, recoverable in-band states sit at h≤0 where the α_safe·h slack
satisfies descent regardless of weak ‖L_g h‖, so the term self-extinguishes (gate_active_frac → ~0).
The h-band change fixed the activation (it fires on the fresh net while h is unshaped), which is why
lg_norm exceeded 0.48 — but this is an activation fix, not a magnitude lever toward the analytic 1.79.

## Verdict 2 — strengthening ∂h/∂v did NOT translate to cps/collision

The representative checkpoint (best.pt @ step 28000, the seed-42 run stopped early at 40k) scores
full-pool cps 0.6995, collision 0.064 — far from SOTA's 0.8852 / 0.006. Two diagnoses establish why,
and they are the durable result of v2.2.1.

### Remaining-collision diagnosis: ∂h/∂v is necessary-looking but not sufficient

Classifying the residual collisions (TREATED vs SOTA): the fast-approach-myopia fraction is essentially
unchanged (SOTA 0.50 → TREATED 0.52) — the bottleneck did NOT move to a ∂h/∂v-immune mode. Critically,
TREATED collides at a HIGHER deployed ‖L_g h‖ (0.39 vs SOTA 0.18) yet still collides: 0.65 gate-∂h/∂v
is insufficient, and the lifted values (e.g. [1.0,1.5): 0.35→0.44; [2.0,2.5): 0.40→0.47) remain far
below the analytic 1.79. Two structural mismatches: (a) ~48% of collisions occur BELOW the gate's speed
floor 0.6 — the gate trained ∂h/∂v at s∈[0.6,2.5] but half the collisions happen slower; (b) the
v2.2.0 mechanism tally on TREATED is 18/21 "box-infeasible-cbf-constraint" — states where no
box-feasible control satisfies descent, which strengthening ‖L_g h‖ cannot fix. No over-braking
(net collision→stuck = −1) and no QP-tightening side-effect (empty-half-space-box 0.021 < SOTA 0.030).

Caveat: TREATED@28000 is a weaker checkpoint than SOTA@34000, so collision COUNTS are confounded by
convergence; class FRACTIONS are the fair before/after signal, and SOTA's n=4 collisions are
high-variance.

### Calibration & ensemble diagnosis: the real bottleneck is h-LEVEL, not ∂h/∂v

This is the key reframing of v2.2.1. An analytic HOCBF is also myopic yet achieves zero collisions,
because it is structurally CONSERVATIVE — so myopia per se does not cause collisions; collisions arise
when V_S FAILS to be conservative (under-estimates h at the states that go on to collide).

TREATED exhibits exactly this: at the last brake-feasible step, 67% of collision-precursors are still
declared SAFE (median deployed h = −0.06); the first danger flag arrives ~16 steps before impact,
after the brakeable window has closed. At the shortest clearance [0,0.1), collision-precursors have
LOWER h than equally-close non-colliding states (coll−safe −0.15 to −0.17) — where it matters most,
V_S labels the precursor more safe. SOTA shows the OPPOSITE: conservative at the critical state (median
h +0.32), flagging ~40 steps early — no calibration failure.

This under-estimation is largely a DATA-COVERAGE GAP: collision-critical (clearance, speed) cells are
~9× less visited than the median cell (62% below median), concentrated at clearance<0.2 and inward
speed 0.3–1.0. This matches the project's recurring failure pattern (a near-optimal policy rarely
visits short-clearance precursors, so max-over-time TD leaves h under-estimated there). The 2-member
ensemble contributes only secondarily: at TREATED critical states, mean-aggregation washes out a
correct danger signal in 24% of cases (deploying MAX instead of MEAN would catch +24%), but 43% are
both-members-wrong — and SOTA's washout is 0% (members agree, conservative). So the mean-aggregation
issue is a weak-checkpoint artifact, not a general property; the durable cause is what V_S learns at
under-visited precursors.

Separately, ~33% of critical collisions already had deployed mean h ≥ 0 (flagged while brakeable) yet
collided — these are the ∂h/∂v control-authority residual, i.e. the minority v2.2.1 actually targeted.

## Verdict 3 — closing the axis and the handoff to v2.2.2

The collision budget decomposes as ~67% calibration under-estimation (V_S sees danger too late) and
~33% control-authority residual (sees it but cannot brake). v2.2.1 addressed only the latter, which is
why it did not move cps/collision. Pushing the same ∂h/∂v axis harder is not the path: the achievable
∂h/∂v fell far short even when lifted, and half the collisions sit outside the gate's speed band. The
evidence-ranked next fix is (C) inject collision-precursor states (short clearance <0.2, inward speed
0.3–1.0) into the value MSE buffer — the most fundamental fix (addresses both the 43% both-wrong and
the 62% under-visited) that generalizes to a converged net; (B) a conservative/asymmetric value loss
penalizing under-estimation as a complement; (A) the mean→max deploy-statistic change is a
weak-checkpoint band-aid only. v2.2.2 will pursue (C) as a single new axis, with L_feas OFF
(cbf_deriv.weight = 0).

## Infrastructure — host-sync reduction & profiling (independent of the L_feas axis)

A numerically-inert refactor moved/batched per-step CPU↔GPU syncs to logging-cadence only, cutting
per-step host syncs ~35 → 3 (the per-step probe forward eliminated on 199/200 steps). Parity is exact
(max abs diff 0.0 on every logged column; verify.sh 35 passed; safety halts fire as before). GPU
utilization rose only modestly, 52% → ~56–58%, and one CPU core remains pinned at 100% — so the logging
syncs were not the dominant limiter.

Profiling established that the rollout is fully GPU-vectorized over all 4096 scenes (BPTT, collection,
HardNet filter, dynamics) with NO per-scene Python loop; the only loop is the time axis (sequential, as
x_{t+1} depends on x_t). The residual one-core saturation is therefore NOT vectorizable — it is
kernel-launch overhead in the sequential 30-step BPTT body (each step fires dozens of small CUDA
kernels through the HardNet filter under create_graph=True), plus a periodic CPU/numpy scene-sampling
burst every 100 steps. The effective levers are graph-capture/torch.compile or op-fusion on the
per-step filter+dynamics body (primary), GPU-side scene sampling (secondary), and dropping the unused
create_graph in the collection filter; bptt_T reduction would help linearly but changes the learning
(a research axis, not infra). multicore is NOT a lever — there is no per-scene loop to parallelize, and
the bottleneck is a single sequential dependency chain. No safe, simple, dramatic speed-up is available
without one of these heavier changes; none were applied here.
